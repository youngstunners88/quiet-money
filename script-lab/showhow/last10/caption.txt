# The last ten years of $500 a month: deposits vs growth, one chart that keeps growing.
== hook | ground: dark
Put in five hundred *dollars* a month
for forty years, and the *last* ten years
alone add about one *million* dollars.
This is an *illustration,* not a promise.
We assume *eight* percent a year,
and no *market* pays that on schedule.
== ten | ground: dark
Here is the *math.*
After *ten* years, you have put in
sixty *thousand* dollars.
It has grown to about *ninety* one thousand.
That feels *slow.*
This is where most people *quit.*
== climb | ground: dark
After *twenty* years, about two hundred
ninety four *thousand* dollars.
Still only one hundred twenty thousand
of it is *yours.*
After *thirty* years, about seven hundred
forty five *thousand.*
So what do the final ten *years* do?
== last | ground: dark
After *forty* years, about one million
seven hundred forty five *thousand.*
Only two hundred forty *thousand*
of it is *deposits.*
Here is the *part* nobody tells you.
The final ten years added *about* one million
*dollars,* from just sixty thousand
of new *deposits.*
== lesson | ground: dark
The first *twenty* years built the base.
The last *ten* harvested it.
*Quitting* at year ten throws that away.
The honest *catch:* real returns bounce around,
fees take a *cut,* and inflation
shrinks every *dollar.*
Your *numbers* will not match this one.
But the *shape* holds.
*Time* does the heavy lifting,
so the move is to *start*
and stay in.
*Follow* for the money rules
school never taught you.
