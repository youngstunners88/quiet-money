# 30-day anchor calendar, 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-19

One anchor Short a day, each pointing at a free lead magnet or the planner. A proposal for `faceless-trends`, not a
post: nothing here is scheduled, and no paid traffic is assumed (the owner has withheld all ad spend).

## How to read it
- **Pillar** is one of the six in `studio.toml [schedule] pillars`. Escape days carry a free magnet only, never the planner (MARKETING.md Rat Race rules).
- **Numbers** come from `python -m faceless.moneymath` (the fact sheet) and are illustrations with their assumptions, not promises. Only 2026-11-14 uses figures; the other rows claim none.
- **Story days are true stories; each has a research brief in channel/research/ (rule 8).** The four story days point at story rows already in the backlog that have no brief yet. Each needs a `faceless-research` brief, and its row's `brief` set, before it runs; this calendar does not write them.
- **Open days** have no anchor: every candidate checked for that pillar was already a made video (`python -m faceless memory` and `faceless.pipeline.ideate.similarity` against `history_texts()`), so the engine's own rotation fills the slot.
- **Tie-in** is the proposal. `lead magnet: <slug>` is linked once the email service is live (slugs: first-quiet-week, monthly-money-snapshot, spending-awareness-30). Until then, and for the planner until its URL is in `[offer] shop_urls`, the offer layer picks the free checklist (rule 13: one offer, one link in the bio).
- **Real people**: only Peña appears (2026-10-25), as the sourced subject of a framework, from his brief in `channel/research/` (rule 8); never as the hook, never quoted, with a "not affiliated" line in the description.
- Every new row was checked to score under 0.55 (the gauntlet's `similarity_max`) against job history and the rest of the backlog.
- Each anchored day's backlog row has the same pillar, topic, angle and hook; the loop map and keywords live here.
