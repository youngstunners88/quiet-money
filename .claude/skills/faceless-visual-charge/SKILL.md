# Visual Charge

## Goal
Make every still feel like a frame from a prestige trailer or high-end editorial, not a calm stock photo with a slow zoom. The voice stays quiet. The image does not have to.

## What "more exciting" means here
| Dimension | Old default | New target |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | Medium, comfortable | Extreme scale or extreme isolation |
| Setting | Generic interior / soft city | Edge of city, night road, industrial edge, weather, height, depth |
| Energy | Static subject + gentle Ken Burns | Implied motion, tension, weather, light that moves |
| Taste | Safe cinematic | Modern, slightly dangerous, editorial |
| Risk | Low visual risk | Controlled visual risk (daring but on-brand) |

## Shot language (use these words in prompts)
Prefer:
- extreme wide, extreme close, low angle, high angle, dutch lean (light), silhouette against weather
- rain-slick street, sodium light, blue hour, overcast pressure, fog cutting a skyline
- lone figure (anonymous, no face), empty highway at night, tower edge, underpass, open water, ridge line
- concrete, glass, steel, wet asphalt, cold fluorescent, single practical light
- scale contrast: tiny human vs huge structure, or huge object filling the frame

Avoid:
- soft desk setups, generic coffee, smiling stock, bright office, clean lifestyle kitchen
- floating money symbols, glowing charts as the hero, neon "success" aesthetics
- anything that looks like a finance-bro thumbnail

## Pillar charge map
| Pillar | Charge direction |
|---|---|
| story | Human-scale drama in real places. Rain, night, institutional buildings, quiet aftermath |
| math | Cold precision + scale. Huge numbers implied by space, not by text. Black, gold, hard light |
| psychology | Interior pressure. Tight framing, isolation, reflective surfaces, slightly off angles |
| myth | Contrast and rupture. Crimson accents, broken symmetry, old belief vs hard fact space |
| playbook | Clean but sharp. Daylight with edge, clear action geometry, no softness |
| escape | Night city, distance, exit routes, elevated views, motion away from the trap |

## Rules that protect the brand
1. No real faces. Anonymous silhouettes or back-of-head only.
2. No text inside the image.
3. Gold stays accent, never the whole mood.
4. Extreme is allowed. Loud is not. Prefer tension over noise.
5. Every beat still must earn its place: if it could be swapped with a soft stock still and nothing is lost, it is too weak.

## How the pipeline uses this
- Image prompts must include at least one charge word from the shot language above.
- Hook stills get the strongest charge. Middle beats can breathe. The final beat must not collapse into softness.
- When Flow clips or motion cards are used, the same charge rules apply: scale, weather, edge, tension.
- Review: flag any still that reads as "safe stock" or "soft lifestyle".
- Flow pictures are made on the owner's free credits (see `faceless-flow`, `faceless-realm-shift`); the browser-pane route is in `faceless-flow-browser`.

## Measurement
A still is upgraded when it would look at home in a prestige trailer stills gallery, a stranger could feel stakes from the image alone before the voice starts, and it is more specific than "cinematic finance still".

## Do not
- Do not turn the channel into hype or guru visuals.
- Do not add faces of real people.
- Do not raise image spend without owner approval.
- Do not abandon pillar colour identity; charge lives inside it.
