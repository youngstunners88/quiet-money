# Cover research brief (Claude Haiku 5.5, 2026-10-12)

Delegated deep dive on what makes planner and finance-planner covers sell, and on the owner's Quinton concept. Model output: facts carry the URLs it gave; opinions are labelled; the list at the end is what it could NOT verify. Re-check a source before relying on it.

## Verified by the model (platform specs)
- Etsy listing photos: at least 2000 px wide and tall; the first photo at least 635 px; files over 1 MB may fail to upload. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347
- Etsy House Rules: AI use must be disclosed in the listing description when an item is made with AI (snippet only read). https://etsy.com/legal/creativity
- Gumroad: covers at least 1280x720, up to 8 covers, each its own shape; "compelling cover images double conversion rates" is Gumroad's own marketing, no method given. https://gumroad.com/help/article/60-adding-a-cover-image
- First Etsy photo shown square on mobile (seller blogs only, not Etsy docs): keep the subject central. https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/etsy-listing-photo-size

## What top listings look like (text only; images not seen)
Keyword-stacked titles; page counts and bundle sizes lead descriptions; finance is usually one tab inside a general planner; finance-only Gumroad planners lead with title and bullets ("calm neutral colors"). Etsy bestseller bundles show hundreds to thousands of reviews at sale prices of about $1 to $3.50: a $17 planner is 5 to 17 times higher, so price is a bigger conversion question than the face. https://www.etsy.com/market/digital_download_planners_bestsellers

## Faces, gaze, trust
- A face gazing toward the product raised attention to it and stated purchase intent in a lab banner-ad study (small fictional shop, one study): https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00881 ; a print-ad eye-tracking study found the same direction (not opened): https://halshs.archives-ouvertes.fr/halshs-01910205v1 . No meta-analysis or effect sizes found.
- AI faces were rated 7.7% more trustworthy than real ones in face-only ratings, and women were rated more trustworthy than men (4.94 vs 4.36 on a 7-point scale): no support in that data for "a man adds trust". https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8872790
- AI disclosure effects are mixed (novelty up, authenticity down in one 2026 paper; no significant effect on purchase intention in a small thesis; perceived manipulation up for AI influencers in a Gen Z study). EU AI Act Article 50 (from 2 August 2026) requires disclosing realistic AI images that could pass as authentic; whether a fictional character counts is a legal question. https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/

## Steepled hands and power poses
No controlled study of steepling found. Power posing: the feeling of power replicates, hormone and behaviour effects do not (Ranehill 2015), p-curve consistent with no true effect (Simmons and Simonsohn 2017), a 2022 meta-analysis finds poser self-perception effects and unclear behaviour effects (sources disagree on study counts). Every positive finding concerns the poser, not a viewer's trust or buying.

## Thumbnails
No study on silhouettes at thumbnail size. Practitioner advice: high contrast, check at about 110 to 200 px. Opinion: a dark figure on near-black with one glowing spot keeps the title and the pin at phone size but may lose the face.

## The model's verdict (opinion)
Keep a visible face; drop the pure silhouette and the "man adds trust" framing; gaze toward the planner is the only supported cue; steeple as a secondary detail; the pin with a sheen rather than a heavy glow; disclose the AI character. Concepts: A Quinton reads the planner (recommended), B hands only, C product-first with no person. Test A against C for 3 to 4 weeks on click-through and favourites per impression.

## Not verified
Cover colours, faces and text density on top listings; Etsy's mobile square crop; Gumroad's thumbnail size; the "double conversion" claim; effect sizes for faces and gaze; any study on steepled hands or on silhouettes at thumbnail size; Körner 2022 study count (88 vs 128); full text of Etsy's AI rule (403); whether Article 50 covers a fictional character; vendor percentages for mockups; any link between dark vs light covers and conversion; any study that a male presenter increases sales.
