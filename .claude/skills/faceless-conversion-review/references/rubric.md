# Conversion-review rubric

Scale: 0 = missing or wrong, 1 = present but weak, 2 = does the job. Quote the page's own words as evidence.
Total out of 20. Nothing here is a measured conversion effect without page-view data.

| # | Criterion | 2 means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clarity of the promise | the first sentence says what the buyer gets and a small action, with no outcome claim |
| 2 | What is inside (true counts) | exact page count, formats, what one page looks like, taken from `python -m faceless planner check` (pdf_*_pages) and LISTING.md |
| 3 | Who it is for, and not for | one sentence names the reader and one names who should look elsewhere |
| 4 | Objections answered (FAQ) | size, printing, starting mid-year, region, advice, devices, files, refund |
| 5 | Proof without fake proof | real sample text, a free sample or a stated method; no invented reviews or counts |
| 6 | Visuals | cover readable at thumbnail size, a flip-through or sample page, an honest "not inside" picture, AI-person mark where needed |
| 7 | Price and refund | the price is plain, the refund route is one calm line, no compare-at price |
| 8 | Disclosures | AI assistance, not financial advice, not medical advice where wellness pages exist |
| 9 | Tone | calm, no hype, no guru words, matches `channel/brand.md` |
| 10 | Next step | one clear button and one free step for people who are not ready (a lead magnet) |

## Output pieces for v2
Title, subtitle, first screen (three lines), five bullets, FAQ (six questions), refund line, disclosure block.
Each piece passes `python -m faceless offer check` before it goes to the owner.
