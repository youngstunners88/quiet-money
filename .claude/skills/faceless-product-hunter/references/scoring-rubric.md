# Product-hunter scoring rubric

Score each candidate 0, 1 or 2 on each line. Write the evidence next to the score, with its date and source. A score
of 2 needs a signal that is not just an ad's age or a seller's own claim. Total out of 14.

| # | Line | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyers exist | no sign anyone pays | one weak sign (ad age, "sold recently" counter) | two independent signs (for example dated reviews on more than one shop, plus a long-running ad) |
| 2 | Recent | nothing newer than a year | some activity this year | reviews or ads from the last 90 days, with dates |
| 3 | Price room | all rivals far below our price, or none | our price sits at the edge of the range | our price sits inside the range rivals charge |
| 4 | Gap | rivals do everything we could | one gap named in 1 to 3 star reviews | a gap named by several reviews that we can fill honestly |
| 5 | We can build it true | needs facts we cannot source, or medical or income claims | buildable with extra checking | buildable from `faceless/moneymath.py` and our own words |
| 6 | Fits our audience | a different audience (for example health) | adjacent | money and habits for 18 to 40, the Quiet Money voice |
| 7 | Reach with no ads | only works with paid traffic | works by search on a marketplace | works by search and by our Shorts and lead magnets |

## Labels
S (the source says it), V (we checked it, and how), U (unverified). Revenue estimates, dashboards and "students made X"
are U and score at most 1 on line 1.

## Automatic stops (overall 0, do not rank)
- A health, medical or income-outcome product.
- Anything aimed at people who cannot tell the content is AI, or who are in distress.
- A candidate whose only evidence is a shown dashboard.
- A product we would have to copy to make.

## Reading the total
12 to 14: a real candidate; propose a small build to the owner. 8 to 11: promising; add one more independent signal first.
Under 8: not proven; log it and move on.
