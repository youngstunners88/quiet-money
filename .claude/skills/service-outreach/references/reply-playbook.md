# Replies (drafted for the owner, never sent by the engine)

Every answer here is a draft the owner reads and sends. Record the reply as an outcome either way
(`service-pipeline`), because replies are the only training signal the loop has.

| They say | Answer with | Never |
|---|---|---|
| "Send examples" | The portfolio index link plus the two or three projects nearest their niche, named. One line, then the ask for a call or a brief. | A pitch deck, or a second proof block |
| "What does it cost?" | That it is quoted per project or monthly, and the two or three things that set the number (count, formats, whether we amplify). Ask for scope. **Then stop and get the owner's number** - the price is theirs (CLAUDE.md rule 9). | Any figure the owner has not given |
| "Do you guarantee views / engagement?" | No, honestly: we do not promise numbers, we produce the work, the client approves every video, and tracking can be set up from day one so they see what it does. | A hedge that implies yes, or a borrowed statistic |
| "Can you pump / shill / make it moon?" | We make promotional content and disclose it; we do not make claims about a token's value or price. If that is the ask, it is a decline. | Any version of yes |
| "Not now / no budget" | One line thanking them, the index link, and that we will leave it there. End the sequence, mark `reply-negative`. | A "circling back in Q2" |
| "Send a proposal" | The named deliverables, the tier shape, the approval step, the AI disclosure, and a request for their timeline. Owner supplies the price before it goes. | A price, a template deck, or promised outcomes |
| "Who have you worked with?" | The verified counts with the as-of date and the named projects from `PROOF.md`. | A client the owner has not cleared, or a view count |
| "Are these AI made?" | Straight answer: production is AI-assisted and that is disclosed; the client approves every video before it posts. | Hiding it, or overclaiming hand-craft |
| Silence | The sequence in `templates/followups.md`, then `dead` for 90 days. | A fourth bump |
| "Stop / unsubscribe" | Nothing further, ever. Record `unsubscribe`, mark the lead `dead` permanently. | Any further message on any channel |

## Escalate to the owner rather than drafting
- Anything asking for a price, a contract, a deadline or a refund.
- Anything asking us to make claims about a token, a yield or a price.
- A complaint, a platform warning, or a legal mention.
- A request to post from the client's own accounts, or to hide the AI disclosure.
