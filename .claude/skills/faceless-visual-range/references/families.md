# Subject and camera families

Use a different family for neighbouring beats. Pair a subject with a camera from another row.

## Subject families (choose concrete nouns, not "a desk")
| Family | Examples that carry money ideas |
|---|---|
| Object | a padlock on a shutter, a brass key on wet asphalt, a single coin on its edge, a fax machine, a vending machine, a parking meter, a metronome, a ladder, a fuse box, a rotary phone |
| Place | a closed shopfront at dawn, a train platform, a loading dock, an empty swimming pool, a courthouse stairs, a laundromat at night, a ferry deck, a market alley |
| Miniature / craft | clay figures at a tiny crossroads, felt hills, cut-paper cliffs, a dollhouse with one lit room, a model railway with one switch |
| Nature | a dam holding back a lake, a river delta from above, ice cracking, a seed splitting stone, ants carrying a crumb, a tide line on sand, a bird on a wire |
| Architecture | a spiral staircase seen from below, a glass tower reflecting another tower, a bridge with one cable highlighted, concrete steps into fog |
| Machine | gears turning once, a clock mechanism, a printing press, a conveyor of identical boxes, a cash drawer sliding shut, an elevator numberless panel |
| Weather / light | rain on a window, fog cutting a skyline, a single beam through dust, a shadow lengthening across a plaza, long exposure of traffic |
| Animal | a crow on a fence, a tortoise on a path, a spider mid web, a heron waiting still |
| Human trace (no face) | hands only, a back view on a platform, a shadow on a wall, footprints in snow, a silhouette behind frosted glass |
| Abstract | a line splitting into two, a dial one notch apart, a stair of stacked blocks, a scale tipping a hair |

## Camera / scale rotation
extreme wide aerial, overhead flat-lay, macro with razor focus, low angle from the ground, eye level through a doorway, long lens compression, silhouette against light, reflection in water or glass, over-the-shoulder with no face, worm's-eye up a tower.

## Beat roles
| Beat | Visual job |
|---|---|
| Hook | the strongest surprise; scale or contrast the viewer has not seen on this channel this week |
| Setup | the place or object where the story starts |
| Number beats | the quantity as an object or a space (a hall of identical doors for 360 payments), not a coin pile |
| "Nobody tells you" | something hidden: a latch, a trapdoor, a seam, an underside |
| Honest catch | a fresh image of risk or friction (a cracked beam, a frayed rope, a narrowing path), **not** a stormy sea |
| Not advice | a quiet human-scale scene or a neutral object |
| CTA | a new calm image with forward motion, never the sunrise highway |
