# Suno playbook (researched 2026-10-12; sources are community blogs, not Suno's docs; check Suno's own pages before relying on a limit)

## Style prompt formula
Genre and subgenre, then mood and energy, vocal character, two or three key instruments with production feel, then tempo in bpm. The newest model weights early words,
so the most important words go first; do not stack synonyms. Keep song-wide identity in the style field and use bracket tags only for changes in one section.
Never name a real artist (also a platform and rights risk); describe the sound.

## Lyrics structure
Tags on their own line, one to three words: [Intro] [Verse 1] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Verse 2] [Bridge] [Final Chorus] [Outro]. Write every chorus out in full; "repeat chorus" is unreliable.
Chorus of 4 plain, singable lines with the title phrase. Concrete images beat abstractions. Respell a mispronounced word in the lyrics box. Character limits for the style and lyrics boxes are
not officially documented in what was found; the desk stays under 250 and 2800 as a safe margin.

## Rights and streaming (unverified secondary sources; the owner must confirm each)
- Free-tier Suno output is reported as non-commercial; paid plans report a commercial licence for songs made while subscribed. Copyright in fully AI output is doubtful (human authorship).
- Distributors screen AI tracks; Spotify allows AI music but targets voice clones and spam and wants AI disclosure; some services report stricter rules. YouTube requires the "altered or synthetic" label.
