# Checking by eye

1. Contact sheet of the bottom 16% of every picture, labelled with the picture number and the decision (crop, keep, needs a person), 4 columns. Read it: that is the ground truth.
2. Write the true list down (which pictures have the handle) before scoring anything.
3. Score each method: right, wrongly flagged, missed. Record cost.
4. After cropping, make a second sheet of the cropped bottoms. A sliver of letter tops means the cut is too low; a poster frame cut off means too high.
5. For a hard case, crop the bottom 10% at full width with red tick marks every 1% of the height; pick the cut from that.
