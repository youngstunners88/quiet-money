## Killed by the attack round

- **X1 Clone best-selling Etsy designs with agents (the StarNet pitch)** (blocked on tos, ip): Copying others' designs is an IP and Etsy-originality problem; StarNet's README never mentions this workflow, only the promo video does.
- **X2 Reverse-engineered Flow automation (gflow-cli) with a saved Google login** (blocked on tos): Unofficial endpoints plus session credentials: ban risk on the Google account the owner uses daily, and brittle. The compliant Flow lane (O4) gets most of the benefit.
- **X3 Agents that create and operate platform accounts (AgentMail inboxes + signups)** (blocked on tos): Platforms ban automated signups and demand phone/ID; account creation stays with the owner. AgentMail/Goshen are only for a support inbox later.
- **X4 Prompt packs as products on Etsy** (blocked on tos): Etsy's creativity standards, as summarised by seller guides, bar selling prompts as the deliverable; verify on Etsy's page before relying on it.

## How a stage moves
idea -> vetted: evidence the platform allows it and the numbers can work. vetted -> built: the asset exists and its numbers are verified. built -> pilot: live at small scale. pilot -> scaled: the metric beat its kill rule for 4 weeks. An item that hits its kill rule is killed and the reason is written here.
