## Description (Markdown that `faceless/shop.py` `md_to_html` supports)

A printable money planner for people who feel behind with money and were never taught the basics. Each day is one page: a picture, a short lesson, one small action and a small tracker. It is undated, so Day 1 is whatever day you open it. No course, no app, no login.

**Who it is for**

- You are 18 to 40 (or just new to this) and nobody ever sat you down and explained how money works.
- You like paper. You want a page to open, ten minutes of work, and a pen.
- You would rather build a habit slowly than read a 300-page book once.

**Who it is not for**

- You want investment picks, tax filing help or advice about your own situation. This book teaches ideas; it does not tell you what to buy.
- You want a spreadsheet that does the math for you. This is a printable PDF.
- You want an app that nudges you. This is paper and a pen.

**What a day looks like**

Every Monday to Friday page has the same five parts, so you always know where you are:

- A black-and-gold picture that matches the day's idea.
- A short lesson in plain English.
- A **Try this** box: one small action, written to fit in about ten minutes.
- A notes area.
- A tiny tracker for what you spent, what you saved or paid down, and how you feel about money (1 to 5).

Each week has a theme and a rhythm: Monday **Learn**, Tuesday **By the numbers**, Wednesday **Do it**, Thursday **Mindset**, Friday **Action**, Saturday a **weekly check-in**, Sunday **reflect and reset**.

**What's inside**

- Two PDF files: US Letter and A4. They hold the same 427 pages each.
- A cover, a welcome page, a **net worth snapshot** with five check-in columns (Days 1, 91, 182, 273 and 365), a **debt map** and a **year at a glance** for all 52 weeks.
- 365 daily pages across 52 weekly themes in four quarters: foundations; debt, credit and compounding; investing basics and income; life, protection and your own freedom number.
- Inside those 365 days, every Saturday is a **weekly check-in** (money in, money spent, money saved or invested, debt paid, three wins and one thing to fix next week) and every Sunday is a **reflect-and-reset** page.
- 52 **Wellness Break** pages on top of the 365, one at the end of each week: an illustrated "Did you know" wellness poster with a line that ties it to a money habit. Education only, not medical advice.
- A summary and goals page, a money words glossary, two notes pages and an about page.

**Topics the weeks cover:** tracking where money goes, the 50/30/20 split, emergency funds, sinking funds, debt payoff, credit basics, compound interest, inflation, index funds, income, taxes in brief, insurance, scams and your own freedom number.

**Read the real thing first**

The best way to decide is to read it. The free **First Quiet Week** is Days 1 to 7 and the first weekly check-in, word for word as they appear in the planner: {{first_quiet_week_url}}

**Start on any day**

The book is undated. Buy it, print Day 1, and begin tonight. Miss a day, skip a day, start in March: nothing breaks. Day 1 asks you to write down what you own and what you owe, and says plainly that a negative number is a starting line, not a verdict.

**Price and refund**

$17 once. No subscription. If it is not for you, ask within 30 days and you get a refund, no questions asked: reply to your Gumroad receipt or message the shop.

**Honest notes**

- This is an educational tool, not financial advice. It never tells you to buy a particular stock, coin or product, and returns are not promised.
- Every number is a worked example with its assumptions stated (hypothetical rates are called hypothetical). Your own numbers are the ones that matter.
- Examples use US-style figures. Where a lesson depends on your country, it says so and points you to an official source.
- Instant PDF download. Nothing is shipped.
- Designed by Quiet Money with AI assistance. The man on the cover is Quinton, the Quiet Money host, a virtual AI-generated character, not a real person.

**Questions**

**1. Is 427 pages too much?**
You never face 427 pages. You face one: today's. The book is built for one page a day, about ten minutes. Print the whole book, or print one week at a time.

**2. Do I have to print it? Can I use a tablet?**
It is made to be printed and written on with a pen, a week at a time if you like. You can also open it in any app that lets you write on a PDF with a stylus, such as GoodNotes or Notability. It has no fillable fields or clickable links, so typing into it will not work.

**3. What if I start mid-year, or miss days?**
It is undated. Open Day 1 whenever you like. If you miss a day, skip it or do it later. The weekly check-in and the Sunday reset are there to bring you back, not to scold you.

**4. Is this only for the US?**
Examples use US-style figures, and the book includes both US Letter and A4 files. When a lesson depends on your country (retirement accounts, tax, borrowing rules), it says so and sends you to an official source for your country. The core ideas (tracking, saving first, interest, debt order) travel.

**5. Will it tell me what to do with my money?**
No, and it is not financial advice. It is education. It explains ideas and asks you to do small tasks with your own numbers. It does not tell you what to buy or what to expect from any investment. For decisions about your own situation, talk to a qualified professional.

**6. What exactly do I get?**
Two PDF files: one for US Letter paper and one for A4. They are the same book. Use whichever fits your printer. There is nothing to install and no account.

**7. What are the Wellness Break pages?**
One a week, 52 in all. Each is an illustrated wellness poster with a line that ties it to a money habit, such as sleep and impulse buys. They are education only and not medical advice. Check with a qualified health professional before changing your diet, exercise or health routine.

**8. I have tried budgeting before and quit. Why is this different?**
It does not ask you to budget every dollar on Day 1. Each page is one short lesson and one small task. Missing a day breaks nothing, and the Saturday check-in is there to pick you back up.
