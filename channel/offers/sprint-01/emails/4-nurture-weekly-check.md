You finished the seven boxes. Now the harder part: making them stick.

The thing that keeps a money habit going is not motivation. It is a time. Pick one weekday and one hour, and put it in your calendar as a repeating appointment. Sunday evening works for many people. Pick yours.

Then each week, in ten minutes, write four numbers on one page: money in, money spent, money saved or invested, and debt paid. Add one win, however small, and one thing to fix next week. That is the whole check.

You do not need an app. A notebook is enough.
