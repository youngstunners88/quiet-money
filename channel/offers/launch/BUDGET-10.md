**Recommended allocation: the whole $10 on one Pinterest test.**
- **Setup:**
  - Campaign spend limit $10, using a lifetime budget. Pinterest's flexible daily budgets can run up to 125% on a single day.
  - Goal: outbound clicks, with a custom maximum bid of **$0.25** a click. The cap builds the break-even rule into the purchase: either clicks arrive at or below the break-even price, or the campaign under-spends and the rest of the money stays unspent.
  - Run 5–7 days, starting now. Late October is cheaper than the weeks around Black Friday.
- **The pin:**
  - Real planner page images or the coin cover, not Quinton (decision D11).
  - The approved line "One lesson. One small action. One picture for every day." plus 427 printable pages, US Letter and A4, $17.
  - The AI-assistance sentence. The copy goes through the phrase gate.
- **Targeting:** keywords such as budget planner printable, money planner, daily planner printable, savings tracker, debt payoff tracker and no spend challenge (check them in Pinterest Trends first). Countries: US, CA, UK, AU.
- **Link:** a Gumroad UTM link (Analytics → Links) pointing at the planner product page: source=pinterest, medium=paid, campaign=test1-2026-10.
- **Who does what:**
  - The owner creates the business and ad accounts and enters the card, about 45–60 minutes.
  - The agent drafts the pin, copy and keywords, and reads sales through quiet-shop `shop_sales`.
  - The owner pastes the Gumroad Links table, about 2 minutes.
  - The agent cannot open ad accounts or launch ads (rules 9 and 11). Windsor.ai has ad-launch tools in this session, but no ad account is connected and launching needs the owner.
- **Fallback if the owner won't open Pinterest this week:** the same $10 and the same rule on YouTube Promote with the "website visits" goal on the IKEA story Short (source=youtube). Setup is about 15 minutes because the channel already exists, and the AI label has to be set by hand.

**What decides whether a second $10 is worth it**
- **The measure:** cost per click = dollars spent ÷ Gumroad's unique clicks on the UTM link. Use Gumroad's count, not Pinterest's. Gumroad credits a purchase to the link if it happens within 7 days. Optionally, adding a free GA4 ID in Gumroad's settings also counts "I want this!" button clicks, an earlier sign of interest.
- **When:** read cost at the end of the run (day 5–7) and sales at the end plus 7 days. A first read fits the Oct 26 brief.
- **Spend the second $10 on the same channel** (new pin or new keywords) if either is true:
  - there is at least 1 sale credited to the link, or
  - clicks cost $0.28 or less, there are at least 30 Gumroad clicks, and at least one "I want this!" click.
- **Stop paid traffic** until the planner page has 100 organic views to judge by if any of these happen:
  - clicks cost more than $0.70 (breaking even would then need a purchase rate above 5%, higher than any benchmark I found),
  - less than $5 is spent at the $0.25 cap,
  - the ad is disapproved or the account is restricted. In that case pause only this channel (the plan's scoped-pause rule, G5).
- **In between, with 0 sales:** the result is inconclusive, so the second $10 does not go to ads.
- **Why the rule leans on cost per click rather than sales:** with 30 clicks and a true 2% purchase rate, zero sales still happens 55% of the time (0.98^30). But a sale is meaningful even at a true 0.4% rate: the chance of one or more in 30 clicks is only about 11%.
- **Spending beyond $20** should wait until 100 or more Gumroad clicks show a cost per sale of $14.01 or less.
