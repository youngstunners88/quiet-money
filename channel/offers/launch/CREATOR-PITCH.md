# Creator pitches (experiment X8)

Owner-gated: you approve the commission and each partner, and you send every note yourself. 10 pitches a week for 3 weeks from Day 8; the agent builds the list (Monid, priced first, about $0.50, or 20 from public search) and drafts one individual note each.

**Who:** small creators (a few thousand to a few hundred thousand followers) whose audience is the same people: budgeting, debt payoff, planners, productivity, "financially behind" 18-40. Real people with real audiences. No giveaway pages, no follower-farm accounts.
**Offer:** a free copy for review plus a commission on sales through their own tagged link (the plan models 40%; set up a Gumroad affiliate link for each partner). Commission is paid only out of sales.
**Rules for both sides**
- They disclose the relationship clearly in the post itself (for example "#ad, I get a commission" or the platform's paid-partnership label). You check every live post within 48 hours.
- They say nothing about income, returns or health results and make no promises; the planner is education only and made with AI assistance. Do not give them copy that says otherwise.
- You never pay for a rating or a review, never script one, and never offer a free copy in exchange for a positive one.
- One note, one follow-up at most, with an easy way to say no. Never repeat a message to someone who declined.

**Note (edit to be personal, one per creator)**
> Hi [name], I like how you [specific thing from a real post]. I have made a printable daily money planner (a lesson and a small action each day, made with AI assistance, education only). I would love to send you a free copy to look at. If you like it and want to share it, I can set you up with your own link and a 40% commission on sales through it; if it is not for you, no problem at all.
