# Warm launch: the first sales come from people who already know you

Plan: `channel/empire/MARKETING.md` (section 6, Days 3-7). Experiment X2: at least 2 sales per 40 messages in 7 days; stop at 0 per 40. Send **one to one, from your own accounts**: no group messages, no bulk text, no email blast. The studio never sends a message in your name.

**Before you send anything**
1. The planner is live and `[offer] shop_urls` holds its real URL (the agent writes it after publishing).
2. Use the tagged link: `<planner URL>?src=dm`. The `src` value comes back with the sale, so the weekly brief can tell these sales from the rest.
3. Only people who would genuinely use it. Do not ask anyone to buy to "help you out", and do not offer anyone a refund-and-keep, a reimbursement or a gift in return for a purchase or a rating: Gumroad bans self-purchases and arranged buying, and a rating from someone you paid is a fake review.
4. 10 messages a day for 4 to 6 days, then stop and read the result.

**Message A (short)**
> Hey [name], I made a printable daily money planner: one short lesson and one small action for every day of the year, 427 pages, US Letter and A4. It is $17 and has a 30-day refund. It was made with AI assistance, and I say so inside. Here is the link if it sounds useful: [link]. No pressure, and tell me to stop if you would rather not get messages about it.

**Message B (for someone who has talked about money stress)**
> You once said money felt like a lot to keep track of. I made something for that: a printable planner with one small money lesson and one action a day, so it stays small. $17, 30-day refund, made with AI assistance (it says so in the book). [link]. Happy to answer questions, and just say if you would rather not hear about it.

**Message C (for someone who prints or journals)**
> Finished a project I would like your honest read on: a 427-page undated money planner you print yourself (Letter and A4). One lesson and one action a day. $17 with a 30-day refund, and AI-assisted (disclosed inside). [link]. If it is not your thing, no worries, and I will not message you about it again.

**If they ask**
- *Is it advice?* No. It is education only and says so; it does not tell anyone what to buy or promise a result.
- *Who made it?* Quiet Money, with AI assistance. The man on the cover, Quinton, is a virtual AI character, not a real person.
- *Refund?* Gumroad's 30-day refund is on the page; you handle refunds yourself (the agent never does).
- *Will it fix my money?* Say what it is: a daily habit book. Never promise an outcome or an amount.

**After a sale:** if the buyer likes it, you may say "a rating on the Gumroad page helps other people find it". Do not ask anyone you gave the planner to, anyone who is related to you, or anyone who was paid, and anyone who rates must say how they know you.
