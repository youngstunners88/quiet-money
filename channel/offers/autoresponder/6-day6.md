Subject: Day 6: stop fee bleeding
Preview: Banks waive fees when you ask.
Send: day 6 after signup

Turn on low-balance and large-purchase alerts in your banking app. Then look at the last 90 days of fees and ask your bank: "Can you waive this as a courtesy?"

Why it matters: one $35 fee a quarter is $140 a year. Alerts stop most of them before they happen.

Today's box: alerts on, and one phone call.
