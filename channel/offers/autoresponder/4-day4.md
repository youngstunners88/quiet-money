List every debt with its interest rate. Put every extra dollar on the highest rate (avalanche) or the smallest balance (snowball). Pick one and stick to it.

Why it matters: $6,000 at 24% paid at a typical minimum takes about 21 years and costs $10,887 in interest. The minimum is not a plan.
