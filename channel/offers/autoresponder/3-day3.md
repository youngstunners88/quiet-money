Subject: Day 3: split every paycheck
Preview: 50 / 30 / 20 in five minutes.

Write your take-home pay. Then split it: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% future.

On $4,000 a month that is $2,000 for needs, $1,200 for wants and $800 for your future. If your needs are over 50%, circle the biggest one. That is next month's project, not today's.

Today's box: the three numbers on one piece of paper.
