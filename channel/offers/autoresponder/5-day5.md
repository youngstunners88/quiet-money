Subject: Day 5: build the $1,000 buffer
Preview: A surprise bill should not become a debt.
Send: day 5 after signup

Sell one thing you do not use, do a no-spend weekend, and park the cash in savings labeled emergencies only.

Why it matters: a $600 surprise put on a 24% card costs about $144 a year in interest while you carry it. A buffer turns a crisis into an inconvenience.

Today's box: the first $100 of the $1,000. Start there.
