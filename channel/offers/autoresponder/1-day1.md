Subject: Day 1: find the leaks
Preview: A $15 subscription is $1,800 over 10 years.
Send: day 1 after signup

Open last month's bank and card statements. Highlight every subscription and recurring charge. Cancel at least one you forgot about.

Why it matters: a $15 a month subscription costs $1,800 over 10 years. Invested instead at an assumed 8% a year, that same $15 a month would be about $2,744. Small leaks are not small.

Today's box: one cancellation. That is the whole job.
