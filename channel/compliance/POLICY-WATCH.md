## Changed: TikTok Creator Rewards Program

- ADDED: Creators must be at least 18 years old (or 19 in South Korea).
- ADDED: Creators must have at least 10,000 followers.
- ADDED: Creators must have at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days.
- ADDED: Creators must have a Personal Account.
- ADDED: Creators must post original content that's eligible for rewards.
- ADDED: Creators must post videos that are at least one minute long.
- ADDED: Videos must be uploaded after joining the Creator Rewards Program.
- ADDED: Videos must have at least 1,000 qualified For You feed views.
- ADDED: Videos must be longer than 5 seconds to count as a view.
- ADDED: Original content is quality content that's designed, filmed, and produced by you.
- ADDED: Content that is reproduced from others with only slight modifications, including videos that are sped up or contain filters, fixed texts, or stickers, is not considered original.
- ADDED: Content that contains different videos or pictures originating from other people, creators, or sources without new and personal ideas is not considered original.
- REMOVED: Post original content that's eligible for rewards.
- REMOVED: Post original and high-quality content that is filmed, designed, and produced entirely by yourself.
- REMOVED: Video views must be longer than 5 seconds.
- REMOVED: Qualified views are unique video views from the For You feed and exclude views with fraud, paid views, disliked views, views with less than 5 seconds watched, promoted views, and artificial views.
- REMOVED: Videos must reach 1,000 For You feed views to start generating earnings.
