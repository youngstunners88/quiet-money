Find your freedom number and the year it arrives.

The Rat Race Escape Planner is a spreadsheet that turns a few honest numbers (what you spend, what you earn, what you invest, what you expect it to grow at) into a plain answer: the invested amount that would cover your spending for good, how many years away it is, and what one extra monthly amount does to that date.

WHAT IS INSIDE (one file, six tabs)
- Start here: five minutes to your first answer
- 1 Your numbers: type your figures; see your freedom number, years to freedom and freedom age
- 2 Income streams: tag every stream Active, Passive or Asset; see dollars per hour and how much of your spending money that works without you already covers
- 3 Rule of 100: the volume math for any offer (100 outreaches a day for 100 days) with a table for your own yes-rate
- 4 Projection: forty years, year by year, in today's dollars, with a chart
- 5 Weekly log: thirteen weeks of tracking, with reply rate, close rate and revenue per hour calculated for you

HOW IT WORKS

2. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.
3. Change the blue cells. The gray cells are formulas.
