One picture, one short lesson and one small action for every day of the year. A 365-day printable money planner that teaches basic personal finance one page at a time.

Each day has its own black-and-gold picture, a plain-English lesson, a Try this action you can finish in about ten minutes, a notes area and a tiny tracker for what you spent and saved. Saturdays are a weekly check-in. Sundays are a reflect-and-reset page.
