## Rules for growth
- Offense stories first: a named person, a decision, a price on each side, what it paid, the honest catch.
- Ruin stories are a one-beat contrast, not a video (they got 4 to 19 views).
- Estimates stay estimates: "Forbes estimates", "reportedly". No net worth without a dated source.
- Every new brief gets 3 or more reputable sources and a "Do not say" list.
