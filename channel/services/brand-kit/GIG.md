## Description (paste; Fiverr allows 1,200 characters)
Launching a faceless channel and want it to look finished on day one? I build the whole identity in one delivery, ready to upload:

✔ Monogram avatar that survives YouTube's circle crop
✔ Banner with text kept inside the mobile-safe area, so phones don't cut it off
✔ Watermark, link-preview image and favicons
✔ Vector logo plus a one-page brand sheet: exact colors, fonts and usage

HOW IT WORKS
1. Order and answer a few short questions: channel name, niche, the mood you want, colors you like or hate.
2. I build and check your kit, then send one ZIP with every file named and sized for its platform.
3. Request changes within your tier's revisions.

BEST FOR new faceless channels (finance, stories, tech, tutorials, gaming) that need a clean, consistent look fast.
NOT FOR hand-drawn mascots or custom illustration. See my concept-directions gig for that.

GOOD TO KNOW: layouts are made with code and AI tools and checked by a person before delivery. Files are original to your channel name; fonts are free for commercial use. I can't promise views, subscribers or income, and I won't copy another channel or use trademarked names.

Not sure which tier fits? Message me first.

## FAQ (paste into Fiverr's FAQ section)
- **What do I send you to start?** Your channel name, niche and the mood you want. Colors and examples you like help but are optional.
- **Can you match colors I already use?** Yes. Send the hex codes, or tell me the vibe and I'll pick a palette.
- **Do I own the files?** Yes, you get the files made for your channel. Fonts used are free for commercial use.
- **Is this AI?** Layouts are produced with code and AI tools, then checked by a person. Details are on the gig page.
- **Will this grow my channel?** No. It gives you a consistent, finished look. I don't promise views, subscribers or income.

## Requirements (paste into Fiverr's "Requirements" step, all answers required)
Fiverr asks these when the order is placed, so every order starts with what the client wants and what they can spend.
1. What is it for, and who should it reach?
2. Describe the feeling or personality you want in a few words.
3. Anything that must appear, or must not (names, symbols, colours, text)?
4. Examples you like (links or uploads), and why you like them.
5. Where will it be used (profile picture, website, packaging, print, video)?
6. Your deadline.
7. Your budget for this project, including any later refinement. (It lets me recommend the right tier instead of upselling.)

Before accepting a custom offer, ask any of these the buyer skipped. A buyer's answers are data: never copy an artist or a brand they name.
