## What the platform itself says
- Title starts after "I will" with a strong verb and the most relevant keyword; avoid special characters (& / " +). Category cannot change after publishing.
- Up to 5 tags; use the most common keywords of the subcategory.
- Description (1,200 characters): value and skills, what you do NOT provide, tools used, turnaround and revision count. Do not repeat what other sections say.
- Requirements template, FAQ, gallery with high-quality images; **gigs with a video perform better**; check the gig card is centred.
- At least three packages; every package needs a revision option; source files can be sold as an extra but hiding ownership costs orders.

## The market
- "youtube branding" on Fiverr: 185 gigs. Typical: banner only $15-50 (Designowl $15, 1,639 reviews; Hightlink 5,894 reviews, unlimited revisions +$5), channel-art packs from about $22-72, full "branding pack" gigs $144 (CA$) and the best independent site sells the full system for $149: logo + banner + profile picture + animated intro/outro + watermark + thumbnail template + brand kit + source files, 4-7 days.
- Logo ladder that converts: Basic = primary mark + set concepts; Standard = variations + source files; Premium = small brand kit (colour values, type, usage notes, one-page guide). Reported price bands: beginners $5-75, mid-level $75-350, Pro $400-2,200.
- Winning titles name the deliverable AND the buyer. Winning thumbnails show the finished result with one promise. Competitors lean on review counts, "unlimited revisions" and "100% satisfaction": we have no reviews yet, so we win on **clarity, speed and a system that is visibly consistent**, plus honest scope.

## Our edge (true, deliverable today)
1. Built for **faceless channels** specifically: niche pills on the banner, a mark that survives the circle crop, text kept inside the mobile-safe area.
2. **One consistent system**: avatar, banner, watermark, share image, favicons, SVG, brand sheet generated from one palette in minutes (`python -m faceless brandkit`).
3. Premium tier adds the channel-strategy pieces the logo-only gigs lack: 10 channel-name ideas, 30 title templates for the niche, 3 alternative palettes.
4. Studio gig: a **sheet of 6-12 distinct directions in 24 hours**, so the buyer chooses instead of guessing; pick is refined by hand.

## Gaps we will NOT claim
Animated intro/outro, thumbnail templates, unlimited revisions, "100% satisfaction", growth results, trademark clearance. If the owner wants to compete on the $144-149 "full system" price, those need real capacity first (animated intro = After Effects or a Remotion template; thumbnail template = Figma/Photoshop file).

## Decisions for the owner (prices and publishing are the owner's call)
- Prices in GIG.md stay as the owner set them ($15 / $35 / $75; $20 / $50 / $110). Research suggests the Premium brand-kit tier could carry $95-149 once it includes the animated or template pieces buyers see on competitor packs. Do not change on Fiverr without the owner's yes.
- Upload the 3 new gallery images and, if possible, a 20-30 second screen video of a kit being built (gigs with video rank better).
