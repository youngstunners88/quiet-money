# Packages and angles (for mid-to-large brands)

**Prices (the owner, 2026-10-23):** a 30-second video is **$65**, a 75-second video is **$120** (`[outbound.prices]` in
studio.toml). They go in replies, quotes and proposals only: a first touch never states a price, it offers a quote, and the outbound
gate holds any draft that does. Bundles and retainers are priced from these per video until the owner sets them (rule 9). The tiers below exist so the models pitch a
*shape* instead of inventing one, and so the owner has something to price.

| Tier | Shape | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Test drop | 1-2 short promos, one round of revisions | A brand that wants to see the work before committing |
| Launch pack | Pre-launch teaser, launch-day promo, post-launch recap, 2 meme clips | Mainnet, listing, token launch, season |
| Monthly retainer | A fixed number of promos + KOL posts a month, one planning call | Projects with a content calendar and a budget line |
| Campaign | Retainer plus coordinated X and Instagram amplification around one event | Raises, partnerships, conference moments |

The showcase's own booking form already names "monthly content retainer" as an option: the retainer is the primary
ask for this audience, with a test drop as the fallback when they hesitate.

## Angles that work on this audience (pick by what research found)
| Signal in the lead card | Angle |
|---|---|
| No video at all, text-and-chart feed | "Your product updates read well and watch like nothing. One 20-second cut per update." |
| Video exists but is screen-recording / AI slop | "The news is good, the edit is costing you the hook. Here is what I would cut differently." |
| Big event dated in the next 6 weeks | "You have one dated moment. A pre / day-of / recap set is three posts, not three weeks." |
| Recent raise or funding news | "You have the budget line and the news; the content calendar is the gap." |
| Active community, no raid assets | "Your holders want something to post. Meme clips and raid-ready cuts are the cheapest reach you own." |
| Strong X, no Instagram | "Instagram Reels is a second audience for the same cut." |

## What the deliverable actually is (be exact in a proposal)
Short vertical video (X and Instagram native), supplied as files plus captions; KOL-style posts from the owner's own
accounts (@youngstunnersss on X, @chris1dragon on Instagram); meme clips and raid assets as separate files. The client
approves every video before it is posted. AI-assisted production is disclosed in the proposal.
