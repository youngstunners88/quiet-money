# Positioning

## Who we sell to (in order)
1. **Mid-to-large Web3 brands** with a marketing budget and a content gap: DeFi protocols, L1/L2 and
   infrastructure projects, gaming and NFT tokens, exchanges and wallets, serious crypto teams.
2. **Funded or recently-raised projects** that have news to amplify (raise, mainnet, listing, season, partnership).
3. **Established projects with weak short-form**: they post text and charts, no video, flat view counts.

Not the focus: micro-caps with no budget, anonymous one-week tokens, anything that asks us to promise price action.

## The offer (keep this language)
- Short viral promo videos for X and Instagram
- KOL-style posts and video shoutouts
- Launch and growth campaigns for Web3 / crypto projects
- Community growth assets (meme clips, raid-ready content)

## TitanX is proof, not the pitch
The 38-project portfolio — TitanX ecosystem included — is **social proof**. It is one line of credibility in a
message, never the subject. Never open with TitanX, never imply a prospect must be in that ecosystem, never write as
if we only serve one chain. Chains we have shipped on: Ethereum, BSC, Base, and the TitanX ecosystem.

## Voice for this audience
Operator to operator. Short sentences. One observation about their actual content, one thing we would make, one ask.
No hype adjectives, no emoji walls, no "gm fam". Premium means specific and brief, not loud.

## What we never say
- No performance promises: views, followers, holders, volume, price, "guaranteed viral", "pump".
- No financial advice and no token recommendations (CLAUDE.md rule 2). We sell content production, not calls.
- No invented numbers. Every figure in a message traces to `PROOF.md`.
- No claim that we worked with a project we did not work with, and no client name the owner has not cleared.
- No fake urgency ("2 slots left") unless it is literally true of the owner's calendar.
- AI disclosure: when a deliverable is AI-assisted, say so in the proposal. It is in the service description, not hidden.
