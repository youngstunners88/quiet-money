# First touch

Three shapes. A model picks one by `channel` and fills the brackets from the lead card. Brackets that cannot be
filled from a sourced field mean the draft is **held**, not softened.

## Shape A - X DM (the default; 55-80 words, no links in message one)
```
[one specific observation about their last post or their feed - what it was about, and what it lacked]

I make short promo video for Web3 projects - [proof block, one line].

[one sentence on the one thing I would make for them, named: e.g. "a 20-second cut of the v3 announcement, hook on the fee change"]

Worth me sending two examples closest to your niche?
```

## Shape B - email to a published partnerships address (90-130 words, subject required)
```
Subject: short video for [project] - [their dated thing or their gap]

[Name / team],

[observation, sourced]

I produce short-form promo video and KOL posts for Web3 projects: [proof block]. [second line of proof only if the
first was a count - never two counts.]

For [project] I would start with [named deliverable]. [If a dated event exists: "Ahead of [event] that would be a
pre / day-of / recap set."]

You approve every video before it posts, and I quote per project before any work starts.

Want the portfolio index and a quote?

[owner signature block]
```

## Shape C - their own business enquiry form (fits a short free-text box)
```
[What you need]: short-form promo video + X/Instagram amplification for [project].
[Why you]: [observation, one line]. [proof block].
[First deliverable]: [named]. Happy to quote per project or monthly.
Portfolio: https://youngstunners88.github.io/titanx/
```

## Rules for every shape
1. One observation, one offer, one ask. Never two asks.
2. The observation must be something a person could only know by looking: a post subject, a dated event, a format
   choice. "I love what you're building" is a rejected draft.
3. One proof block from `PROOF.md`. No invented numbers, no view counts.
4. No promise of results, no price, no token talk, no financial advice.
5. No links in an X DM (they cost reach and read as spam); the showcase link goes in the reply.
6. Name the project the way they write it, ticker and casing included.
7. Never mention TitanX first, and never imply they need to be in that ecosystem.
