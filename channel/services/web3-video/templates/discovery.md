# Discovery (the first reply to any lead who answers)

Ask before you quote. A lead who answers these is telling you what to make and what they can spend; one who will not answer
budget is usually not a client yet. Send the questions in one message, short, numbered, easy to answer from a phone.

---

Thanks for getting back to me. So I make exactly what you need, a few quick questions:

1. **What is it for?** A launch, an update, an event, an explainer, or ongoing content?
2. **Who should it reach?** New users, your current community, investors, partners?
3. **Where will it run?** X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, your site? (This sets the size and length.)
4. **Length:** a 30-second clip or a 75-second one? Or a few of them?
5. **Must-haves:** key message, links, product shots, a call to action, anything that must or must not appear?
6. **Look and voice:** any videos you like (yours or others) and your brand assets (logo, colours, fonts)?
7. **Timing:** when do you need it live?
8. **Budget:** what budget do you have in mind for this? (For reference, a 30-second video is $65 and a 75-second one is $120.)

Once I have these I'll send a short script outline before anything is made.

---

How to use the answers:
- Budget fits one video: quote it and offer the script outline. Budget fits several: propose a set (still priced per video from
  `[outbound.prices]`; a bundle or retainer price is the owner's to set).
- Budget is below $65: offer the 30-second video as the smallest step; never cut the price without the owner's yes.
- No budget answer after one follow-up: record `reply-question` and move on.
- Never promise views, followers, holders or price action; no token talk (rule 2).
- Record what came back: `python -m faceless outbound outcome <slug> reply-positive|reply-question|call-booked|closed`.
