## Why this niche

Money psychology + personal finance was chosen for **income soonest**:

1. **Highest ad rates in short-form.** Finance is the top-RPM Shorts/TikTok category (finance and AI-tools
   accounts report ~$0.60-1.20 RPM in TikTok Creator Rewards vs ~$0.40 average; long-form finance RPM is $10-18).
2. **Money before monetization thresholds.** Finance has the richest affiliate market ($7-$150 per sign-up
   for budgeting apps, brokerages, high-yield savings, credit tools). A link in bio earns from video #1,
   long before the 10k-follower / YPP thresholds.
3. **Evergreen + endless.** Stories, math, psychology, myths, and playbooks never run out and don't expire.
4. **Fits still images + voiceover.** The format doesn't need motion video or a face to be premium.
5. **Global.** Money psychology travels across countries; numbers are stated as US dollars with clear assumptions.
