# Compliance: what keeps the channel monetizable

## YouTube "inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious content", July 15 2025)
Mass-produced, templated, near-identical uploads lose monetization. Strikes: warning, 90-day suspension, removal.
What keeps us eligible:
- **Original value in every video:** a real story, an exact calculation, a named bias with a counter-move.
- **Real variation:** five pillars with different structures, visual styles, color grades, and music keys.
- **Originality gate:** the gauntlet blocks any script too similar to an earlier one (`similarity_max`).
- **No reading Wikipedia aloud:** scripts are written for the ear with an angle, not copied text.

## AI disclosure
- TikTok: turn on the "AI-generated content" label (Upload-Post sends `tiktok_is_ai_generated=true`).
- YouTube: "altered or synthetic content" = yes (`containsSyntheticMedia=true`).
- Every description: "Narration and visuals are AI-assisted."

## Finance content
- Every description: "Educational content, not financial advice."
- Never: "buy X", ticker picks, "guaranteed", "risk-free", "can't lose", promised returns.
- Projections always state the assumption ("at 8% a year") and come from `moneymath.py`.
- Stories use documented facts only; sources are recorded in each script's `facts` list.

## Affiliate disclosure (FTC and platform rules)
- Descriptions with links say: "Some links are affiliate links: I may earn a commission at no cost to you."
- Use TikTok's "promotional content" toggle when a video itself promotes an offer.

## Real people
- Never depict a real person's face (no likeness fakes). Hands, silhouettes, objects, places only.
- Only documented, public facts about them; no speculation about private lives.

## Music
- Procedural, original beds generated per video: no copyright claims, no Content ID.
- Trending sounds may be added manually in the TikTok app at low volume (they're licensed in-app).
