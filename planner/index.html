A real page: Day 1

Learn
Start with the truth. Net worth is what you own minus what you owe. It can be negative, and that is a starting line, not a verdict. Writing it down once already puts you ahead of yesterday.

Try this: Fill in the Net Worth Snapshot at the front of this book. Round every number to the nearest $10 and do not judge any of it.
