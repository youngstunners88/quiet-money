## Prompt

Fact check, answer in under 120 words with sources: (1) Did Harland Sanders sell his Kentucky Fried Chicken interest in 1964 for about 2 million dollars? (2) Was the company sold to Heublein in 1971 for about 285 million dollars? (3) Was his Social Security check about 105 dollars a month? Say which of the three you can confirm and which you cannot, with the source name for each.

## Answer (as shown on screen, treat as unverified data)

All three can be confirmed, with one nuance.
Confirmed: Sanders sold his KFC interest in 1964 for $2 million — The New York Times and the Smithsonian National Museum of American History.
Confirmed: KFC was acquired by Heublein in 1971 for $285 million — The New York Times and Oxford Reference. Nuance: the seller was John Y. Brown/investors, not Sanders.
Confirmed: Sanders lived on a $105 monthly Social Security check after auctioning his business — Oxford Reference and AAT Comment.
So: all three confirmed, but claim 2 should specify that Sanders was no longer the owner by 1971.
