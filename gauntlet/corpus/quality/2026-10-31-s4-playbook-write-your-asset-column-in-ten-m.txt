Most people cannot name one thing they own that pays them every month. Your paycheck funds today, but what actually funds your life when you stop working? Most think their house or car is an asset, but if it drains cash, it's a liability. So how do you build an asset column that pays you, starting in ten minutes? Step one. Open a blank note on your phone titled My Asset Column. List everything you own that deposits cash into your bank each month, even if it's $0. Step two. Add one target line item, like $100 a month into an 8% index fund. What does writing down a tiny $100 deposit actually build over time? At 8% a year, $100 a month invested for 30 years becomes $149,036 from $36,000 put in. Step three. Automate that transfer right now so your asset column grows on its own. Real talk: passive income takes years of deliberate work, not overnight magic. Once your asset column pays your monthly bills, you don't need a job to survive.
