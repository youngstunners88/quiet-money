He says he had one thousand thirty six dollars left in the bank. Then he sold two thirds of his company. That man is Alex Hormozi. The low point he describes was December twenty sixteen. So what changed? Not luck. A method, and years of work. He built a company that taught gym owners how to sell memberships, then sold the same customers supplements. Here is the part nobody tells you. He did not chase strangers. He found a hungry group and kept serving it. In twenty twenty one, he says, he sold a sixty six percent stake to a private equity firm, in cash. That put the value of the two companies at forty six point two million dollars, he says. He kept the last third, and later started a firm that buys stakes in other businesses. Now the honest catch. About one in five new US businesses close within a year, according to government data. And by his own telling, it took years of long weeks and failures. Nobody can promise yours. So what is the takeaway? Pick one group with a clear problem, and solve it better than anyone around you. That is a method, not a miracle. Test it small before you bet anything big. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
