Your six thousand dollar credit card balance takes two hundred fifty-two months to clear. That is twenty-one years of minimum payments at twenty-four percent annual percentage rate. Stay until the end to see the exact cost of this quiet trap. Most people look at the minimum due and assume the bank is just helping them out. That minimum is calculated as one percent of the balance plus the monthly interest. But here is the part nobody mentions about how debt actually compounds. At twenty-four percent interest, debt doubles every three years. By the time you finish paying off that six thousand dollars, you hand over ten thousand eight hundred eighty-seven dollars in interest alone. That is nearly double the original purchase just for borrowing time. Now compare that to paying three hundred dollars a month instead. The timeline drops from twenty-one years down to just twenty-six months. Total interest plummets to one thousand seven hundred thirty-nine dollars.
