Leaving your cash sitting safely in a traditional bank account is a guaranteed way to lose a third of your life's savings without spending a dime. It feels secure to watch that balance sit untouched year after year in a low-yield account. How much purchasing power quietly vanishes when you leave $10,000 sitting at a 0.01% interest rate? Most people assume a safe bank balance stays worth the exact same amount over time. That is the illusion keeping millions of people trapped. At a historical 3% inflation rate, look at what happens to your buying power. Think about how $100 today is worth just $55 in buying power after 20 years of 3% inflation. When you apply that same math to $10,000 over 15 years, the loss is staggering. The bank pays you basically zero at 0.01%, while inflation eats away at your real wealth every single day. Your $10,000 still says $10,000 on the screen, but it buys what $6,400 used to buy. That is a silent tax on every dollar you refuse to put to work. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
