A budget isn't a sign you're broke. It's a plan with your name on it. Most people think writing down their spend is a punishment for running low on cash. They assume high earners just spend freely and never look at a single bank statement. So why do millionaires track every dollar that enters and exits their bank account? Without a clear target, unguided money disappears into forgotten habits and unseen subscription fees. A simple $15/month subscription alone quietly drains $1,800 over 10 years. What happens when you give those exact dollars a job instead of letting them leak out? Investing $500/month at 8%/yr builds $745,180 over 30 years. That math doesn't happen by accident. It happens because a budget bought that future freedom first. A budget isn't restriction. It's controlling your velocity, whether you make $30,000 or $300,000. Your counter-move is simple: write one single page at the start of every month. Tell every single dollar where to go before your impulses make the decision for you. Follow for the money rules school never taught you, so you stay ahead.
