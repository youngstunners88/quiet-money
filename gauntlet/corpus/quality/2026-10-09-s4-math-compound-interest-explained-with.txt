Every single rotation pulls in a wall of snow. Between year 30 and year 40, your balance explodes all the way to $1,745,504. That final decade alone created over $1,000,000 of profit, completely crushing your first 30 years combined. The real fortune isn't built early. It's collected by refusing to step off the ride.
