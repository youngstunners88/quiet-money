A 2% cashback credit card can easily cost you hundreds every year. Here's the exact step-by-step math credit card companies rely on you completely ignoring. Imagine you put $2,000 every month on a standard 2% cashback card. You earn $40 back every single month, totaling $480 in annual rewards. That feels like free money landing right back into your bank account. But here's the part nobody mentions about keeping even a small balance. Suppose you carry a minor $1,500 unpaid balance at a normal 22% APR. That $1,500 alone generates $330 in annual interest charges straight to the bank. Worse yet, carrying that balance instantly wipes out your interest-free grace period. Now your new $2,000 monthly spending accrues immediate interest, adding $440 more per year. Your total interest bill hits $770, completely destroying your $480 in cash back rewards. You didn't make $480, you actually paid the bank $290 for spending your own money. Always clear your statement balance in full every month, or don't play their rewards game. Follow Quiet Money for the money rules school never taught you.
