Old merchants can quietly pull money from your PayPal balance without ever asking your permission again. When you bought that one-off trial years ago, you likely granted permanent automatic billing authorization. Step one. Log into PayPal on a web browser, click the gear icon, and choose Account Settings. The phone app hides this menu, so a desktop browser or mobile web view works best. Step two. Click Money, Banks and Cards on the left, then scroll down to Automatic Payments. But here's the part nobody mentions. Every vendor you authorized over the years is still active. You'll likely spot at least one or two recurring charges you forgot about years ago. A $15/month subscription forgotten for 10 years drains $1,800 straight out of your pocket. Step three. Click on each outdated vendor name, select Cancel, and confirm Automatic Billing Cancellation. That instantly revokes their digital token so they can't charge your backup bank account ever again. Take 5 minutes to audit this list twice a year to protect your hard-earned cash. Follow for the money rules school never taught you, and stop silent cash drains today.
