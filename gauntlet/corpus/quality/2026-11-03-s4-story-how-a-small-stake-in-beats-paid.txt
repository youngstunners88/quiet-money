In 2008, LeBron James took a small piece of a headphone start-up for promoting it. Most athletes wanted a fast paycheck, but he chose equity instead of upfront cash. In 2014, Apple bought that headphone company for $3B. So how much did he actually pocket? Most people assume a $3B tech buyout meant he walked away with hundreds of millions. But his reported payout was about $30M in cash and Apple stock. That was still reported as the largest payout to a pro athlete at the time. So why don't all creators and employees demand equity instead of taking wages? Here's the catch: most promotional start-up equity stakes end up paying out zero dollars. He could take that risk because his guaranteed paycheck already covered all his expenses. Equity is how wealth accelerates, but steady cash flow is what keeps you in the game.
