Society tells you any college degree is a guaranteed financial asset. That's a lie. Here's what they don't teach you. A degree without market demand functions as an expensive liability. Let's look at the math of opportunity cost. Spending 4 years out of the workforce destroys your early compounding. If you invest just $200 a month at 8% a year starting at 25, you hit $698,202 by 65. Wait until 35 with that same $200 a month, and you only reach $298,072. That 10-year delay costs you $400,130 in lost growth. That's the real price of a slow start. But here's the part nobody mentions. Unfocused degrees pile on heavy debt without boosting your earning power. If you borrow $6,000 at 24% APR and pay only the minimum, you stay trapped for 252 months. You'll waste $10,887 in interest alone on that balance. That's money vanishing into thin air. The rule is simple. Treat education like a business. Demand a clear, measurable return on your capital. Don't buy credentials blindly. Buy skills that the market pays for right now. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
