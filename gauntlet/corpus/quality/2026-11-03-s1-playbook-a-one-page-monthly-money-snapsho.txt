Two lists and one subtraction tell you if you're ahead or behind. Most people track daily coffee purchases, but miss the one number that actually dictates their freedom. You don't need complicated budget software or hours of painful expense tagging to see reality. Step one. Open a blank note on your phone or grab a single index card. List everything you own that has real cash value today. Cash, savings, and investments. Step two. Right underneath, list every single dollar of debt you owe right now. Add up your credit cards, student loans, and car balances into one blunt total. Step three. Subtract what you owe from what you own to find your net worth. Repeat this simple subtraction on the first day of every month to track your trajectory. Drifting for 10 years without seeing this number can quietly devastate your long-term wealth. Investing $200/month at 8% from age 25 to 65 yields $698,202. Starting at 35 yields just $298,072. That 10 years of drifting costs $400,130. Knowing your single monthly number ensures you catch leaks before decades slip away. Follow Quiet Money for the money rules school never taught you.
