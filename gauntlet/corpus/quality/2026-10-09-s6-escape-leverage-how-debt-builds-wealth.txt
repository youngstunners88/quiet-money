A 20% drop in price can instantly wipe out 100% of your wealth. Here's the exact math of how leverage quietly builds massive empires and suddenly bankrupts them. Say you buy a $100,000 property. You put down $20,000 and borrow the other $80,000. That's 5x leverage, meaning you control a massive asset with a very small deposit. If the market goes up 20%, the property is now worth $120,000. You just doubled your $20,000 investment, turning a small win into a massive victory. But here's the part nobody mentions about the dark side of using borrowed money. If the property value drops by 20%, your entire $20,000 down payment is completely gone. The bank still wants their $80,000, and you're left holding a completely empty pocket. This double-edged sword is why even famous real estate gurus have faced massive corporate bankruptcies. This week, calculate the leverage on your own home or any asset you want to buy.
