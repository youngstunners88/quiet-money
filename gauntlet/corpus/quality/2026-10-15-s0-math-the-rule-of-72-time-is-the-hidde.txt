Here's the math banks don't want you to calculate. It's called the Rule of 72, and it explains how wealth actually builds. Divide 72 by your investment return, and you get the years it takes to double. At 8% a year, your money doubles every 9 years without extra work. Start at 25 investing $200 a month at 8%, and you reach $698,202 by 65. Wait until 35 with that exact same plan, and you drop to $298,072. That ten-year delay costs you $400,130 in final wealth. But here's the part nobody mentions. The rule works against debt too. At 24% credit card APR, debt doubles every 3 years. A $6,000 balance paid with minimums turns into 21 years and $10,887 in interest. Time is the hidden multiplier of everything you own or owe. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
