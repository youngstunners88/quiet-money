That free app trial you downloaded for one weekend just billed you $49. How did a zero dollar trial turn into a monthly charge on your card? Most people assume app stores send a warning text before the trial ends. They don't. The billing cycle starts the second the trial clock hits zero. Where is that money hiding right now inside your mobile device settings? Step one. Open your settings app, tap your Apple ID or Google profile at the top. Step two. Tap subscriptions to view every active and expired service linked to your card. Step three. Tap the rogue trial and select cancel subscription before the next renewal date. That small $15 monthly subscription for 10 years equals $1,800 gone. Kill those forgotten trials today and keep that cash in your checking account.
