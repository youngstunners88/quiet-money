A $200 monthly habit beat a $10,000 windfall, and time was the reason. Here's the setup, one person drops $10,000 in once, then never touches it again. Another person adds $200 a month, small, boring, easy to forget, for 30 years. After 30 years, the $10,000 lump sum grows to $76,123, which sounds pretty great. But the $200 a month grows to $243,994, leaving the windfall far behind. And here's the strange part, that person only paid in $72,000 of their own money. So most of that pile wasn't saved, it was grown, by time doing quiet work. Time works through doubling, the Rule of 72, at 8%, money doubles in 9 years. Which means waiting 9 years isn't a small delay, it's losing a whole doubling forever. Meanwhile waiting isn't neutral, at 3% inflation, $100 is worth $55 after 20 years. So starting small beats starting perfect, because the clock counts every month you're in. Today, pick any amount you won't miss, and let time start its first doubling.
