Swiping a credit card actively numbs the specific pain center inside your brain. A famous MIT study proved card buyers willingly bid up to 100% more than cash buyers. When you hold physical paper cash, parting with it triggers mild, actual discomfort in your mind. Psychologists call this the pain of paying, and it's your natural, biological spending brake. But modern credit cards and digital wallets completely bypass this biological friction. But here's the part nobody mentions: the easier it is to pay, the less you care. Features like Apple Pay and face scans make parting with your money entirely invisible. You're not careless; your brain simply doesn't register that you've lost anything at all. To protect your wealth, you must build the transaction friction back manually. Step one: delete all saved credit card details from your phone and favorite shopping apps.
