Moving your credit card debt to a 0% card sounds like a cheat code. It is not free money. The fine print hides a sting. How much does that upfront balance transfer fee actually cost you? Most people assume a 0% APR offer buys you pure savings for a year. They forget about the 5% fee charged on the total amount you move over. If you transfer $6,000 onto that new card, the bank takes $300 instantly. Can you pay off that full balance before the zero percent period expires? If you pay $300 a month on a $6,000 balance at 24% APR, it takes 26 months. That same $6,000 card racks up $1,739 in interest over those 26 months. If your transfer fee is $300 and you clear the debt in time, you win. But miss the deadline, and standard APR kicks in on the remaining balance. Run the exact math before you move a single dollar.
