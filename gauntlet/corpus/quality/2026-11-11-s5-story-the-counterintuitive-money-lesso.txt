He earned more than movie stars in his prime, but when he died, he couldn't afford his own funeral. That was the shocking reality for Billy Eckstine, a musical giant who packed stadiums across America in the 1940s. So where did every single dollar vanish to? You'd assume he blew it all on wild parties and luxury cars. The truth is far worse. He confused massive gross revenue with actual net wealth. He funded a massive touring big band out of his own pocket every single week. Payroll, travel, and stage production ate every cent before he ever saw a paycheck. Even worse, he didn't own the masters to the records that made him famous. So why do so many high earners still repeat this exact same cash flow trap today? Top-line revenue is just a vanity metric. Real wealth is what stays in your control after all the bills clear. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
