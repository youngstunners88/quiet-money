Your salary has a hard ceiling, but business owners look at dozens of deals a week. That constant stream of options is called deal flow, and it's why owners build real fortunes. Employees wait for one annual raise. Owners review ten new opportunities every single month. By reviewing hundreds of offers, they spot the single asymmetric bet that changes everything. You can't get that kind of volume while trapped inside a 9-to-5 routine. That's why big outcomes come from seeing many deals, not from earning one bigger salary. But here's the honest catch about this game. Using other people's money and outside partners means deals can fail and debt is real. You owe it back or you give up equity when things go wrong. That exposure scares most people right back into a safe paycheck. Start small this week by analyzing three local business offerings just for practice.
