That store sign promises 0% interest for 12 months. It sounds like free money. It isn't. Here is the trap they hide in the fine print. Miss the payoff deadline by one single day. You do not just pay interest on what is left. They back-charge it on the full original purchase price. Imagine buying a $6,000 credit card balance or a large appliance on deferred terms. You pay down most of it. Only $100 remains when the promo period ends. Because of that tiny leftover sum, retroactive interest triggers at rates like 24% on the entire original amount. At 24%, debt doubles every 3 years according to the rule of 72. That mistake costs you dearly. Banks count on you slipping up. They bake this exact penalty into their profit model. Treat every promotional offer like a ticking clock. Set your own deadline two weeks early. Never leave a balance to chance. Pay it off completely before the clock runs out.
