You're told every dollar of debt is financial poison that keeps you broke. Yet the ultra-wealthy deliberately borrow millions of dollars to expand their net worth. Why would people who already have millions choose to take on debt? Most assume all borrowing traps you, but the danger isn't borrowing. It's what you buy. Consumer debt on depreciating stuff crushes you. At 24%, debt doubles every 3 years. So how do the rich turn that exact same math into an advantage? They use productive leverage. They borrow to acquire cash-flowing assets that outpace the interest cost. Consider a $100,000 property bought with $20,000 down. That's 5x leverage. If that property's value rises 20%, you double your $20,000 down payment before costs and interest. What happens to that leverage if the market drops instead of rising? A 20% price drop completely wipes out your entire $20,000 down payment. That's why the wealthy treat debt like fire: powerful fuel when controlled, fatal when reckless. Bad debt buys things that lose value. Good debt finances assets that pay you back. Debt isn't your enemy. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
