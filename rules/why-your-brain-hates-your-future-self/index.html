The full story

Your brain treats your future self like a total stranger. That's why you prefer $100 today over $150 next year. It's called present bias. It quietly sabotages your wealth every single day. When you picture yourself at 65, brain scans show it lights up the exact same way it does when you think about a stranger. You're literally robbing a stranger you haven't met yet. But here's the part nobody mentions.

Waiting 10 years to start costs you a fortune. Starting at 25 versus 35 with $200 a month at 8% until 65 creates a massive gap. That 10-year delay costs you $400,130. The math doesn't care about your feelings. So how do you trick a brain wired for immediate survival? You stop relying on willpower entirely. Willpower fails every time.

Instead, you make your future self visible. And you automate the entire process. Set a transfer for payday. Send the money away before you even see it in your checking account. Out of sight means out of reach for your present bias. Fix your system once, and let time do the heavy lifting.
