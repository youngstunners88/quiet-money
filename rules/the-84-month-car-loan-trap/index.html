The 84-Month Car Loan Trap

Exposing how extending a car loan to 84 months doubles total interest paid on a depreciating asset while robbing you of compounding returns.

The rule: Extending your car loan doubles the interest.

Key numbers

  • ASSET VS LIABILITY
  • $149,036
  • $36,000
  • STEP ONE

The full story

Spreading a car loan across 84 months looks like a smart monthly discount. It isn't. Here is the exact math banks hide from you. When you double the length of the loan, you double the total interest paid on a depreciating asset. The vehicle loses value the moment it leaves the lot. Meanwhile, your interest payments keep climbing.

You end up paying thousands extra for a machine that is rusting away. That extra cash could work for you instead. For example, $100 a month invested for 30 years at 8% annual return grows to $149,036. You only put in $36,000 of your own money over that timeline.

Compound interest builds wealth when you let time do the work. A long car loan does the exact opposite. It steals your future buying power. Keep your loan terms short and keep your cash where it grows. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
