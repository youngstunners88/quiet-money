The full story

Your brain categorizes money into invisible buckets. That's why a tax refund vanishes into thin air. It's called mental accounting. You treat earned pay and bonus cash differently, even though spends are identical. A paycheck feels hard-fought. A tax refund or a work bonus feels like free lottery winnings. So you spend it on dinners, gadgets, or weekend trips you'd never buy with regular salary cash.

Here's the part nobody mentions. That random cash leak stops you from building real wealth. If you took that $500 extra a month and invested it at 8% a year for 30 years... You wouldn't have a temporary gadget. You'd build up to $745,180 over time. That's the power of treating every single dollar the exact same way in your budget.
