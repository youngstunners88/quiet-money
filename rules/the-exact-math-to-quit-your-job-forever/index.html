The full story

$3,000 a month of passive income needs about $900,000 invested. That number comes from the 4% rule, a classic guideline from the 1998 Trinity study. It is not a guarantee, but it is the exact math people use to replace a paycheck. If you invest $500 a month at 8% a year, it takes 32.2 years to hit that target.

Double your monthly deposit to $1,000, and that timeline drops to 24.4 years. Push it to $2,000 a month, and you cut the wait down to 17.4 years. But here is the part nobody mentions about escaping the rat race. You are trading current comfort for future freedom.
