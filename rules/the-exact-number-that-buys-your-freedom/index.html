The full story

You're trapped in the rat race because your paycheck stops the second you stop working. Here's the math that sets you free, using the 4% rule from the 1998 Trinity study. To replace $2,000 of monthly expenses with passive income, you need about $600,000 invested. That sounds impossible until you look at how time and compounding work for you.

Investing $500 a month at 8% a year reaches that $600,000 target in 27.6 years. Up that to $1,000 a month, and you hit it in 20.2 years. But here's the catch nobody mentions. Passive income takes serious capital or years of discipline. Real estate has vacancies, and stocks drop when you least expect them.
