The full story

Most beginners kill their first winning side project just before it actually pays them. There's a quiet three-step rule that turns one small win into a massive lifetime payoff. According to business builder Alex Hormozi, we fail because we crave novelty over consistency. He calls it the More, Better, New framework. First, you've got to do more of what works.

If sending ten cold emails got you one client, send one hundred before changing your offer. But here's the part nobody mentions. Most new ventures fail in the first few months. About 1 in 5 US businesses close in their first year, according to federal labor statistics. Once you do more, make it better. Optimize your current process before launching a second product.

Only when you've maximized the old do you launch something new. Don't try doing all three. An extra $1,000 a month from one optimized side income, invested at 8% for 10 years, becomes $182,946. Keep your focus sharp. Follow for the money rules school never taught you. Stop jumping to the next shiny trend before squeezing the value from your current win...
