The full story

Trading your time for money is a mathematical trap designed to keep you permanently broke. A legendary wealth framework from 2000 maps this trap into four quadrants, showing you the exact exit. The first quadrant is the employee. You trade your limited hours for a flat day's pay. The second is self-employed. You own a job, but if you take a week off, your income stops instantly.

But here's the part nobody mentions. Both of these quadrants require your physical body to earn a dollar. To build actual freedom, you must cross over to the right side of the wealth map. The third path is the business owner, who builds scalable systems that earn while they sleep. The fourth path is the investor, who uses passive assets to do all the heavy lifting.

To secure $2,000 a month of passive income, you need about $600,000 invested. But here's the catch. Building that capital takes years of hard work, and leverage can ruin you.
