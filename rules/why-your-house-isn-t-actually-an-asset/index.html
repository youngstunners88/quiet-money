The full story

Your home isn't an asset. Robert Kiyosaki wrote in Rich Dad Poor Dad that it's actually a liability. Here's why. An asset puts money in your pocket. A liability takes money out. Your house takes cash every single month for mortgage, taxes, and repairs. It doesn't pay you. Most people stay stuck in the rat race because they buy a bigger house as soon as they get a raise.

Expenses rise right along with income. You're forced to keep working just to pay the bills. Kiyosaki says true wealth comes from buying things that generate cash flow without your labor. But here's the honest catch. Real estate takes heavy capital, and Kiyosaki's own company once filed for bankruptcy. Leverage cuts both ways. Debt can break you just as fast as it builds you.
