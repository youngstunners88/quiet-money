The full story

A $6,000 balance can cost you $10,887 in interest alone. Most people think making the minimum payment protects them. It doesn't. Here's the math your bank statement hides in the fine print. Imagine you owe $6,000 on a card at 24% APR.

Your bank calculates the minimum as 1% of the balance plus monthly interest. If you pay only that required minimum, it takes 252 months to clear. That's 21 years of your life spent paying off one purchase. You end up handing the bank $10,887 in interest charges.

But here's the part nobody mentions. If you lock your payment at $300 a month instead, everything changes. The exact same debt gets wiped clean in just 26 months. Your total interest drops to $1,739. You save over $9,000.
