The full story

Your rent isn't throwing money away. It's the maximum you'll pay for housing. A mortgage is the minimum you'll pay every month. But here's the part nobody mentions. Homeownership comes with hidden costs that vanish into thin air. Property taxes, roof repairs, and interest don't build equity. They're dead expenses.

When you rent, your fixed payment buys you absolute freedom and flexibility. Meanwhile, imagine putting just $200 a month into a standard market index. At 8% a year, that small habit grows into a massive financial cushion. Over 40 years, that routine turns into $698,202 sitting in your account.
