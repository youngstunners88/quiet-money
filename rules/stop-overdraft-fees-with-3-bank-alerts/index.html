The full story

Stop paying your bank 35 dollar fees for mistakes you can fix today. Banks love low balances because it prints cash for them. You are going to shut that down. Step one. Open your mobile app and find the alert settings right now. Turn on low balance alerts and set the trigger to 100 dollars.

That gives you a buffer before your card gets declined at the register. Step two. Set a large purchase alert for anything over 50 dollars. This catches impulse buys the exact second you tap your plastic. Step three. Turn on deposit alerts so you know when your paycheck actually clears.

That stops you from guessing your cash flow during tight weeks. But here is the part most people miss. Alerts do not just warn you. They rewire your brain to pay attention to numbers. Take five minutes to set these up today and keep your hard earned cash where it belongs
