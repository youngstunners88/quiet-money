The full story

Being broke doesn't just drain your bank account. It costs you a fortune in hidden time taxes. When you're constantly stressed about cash, your brain makes expensive survival choices. You pay massive convenience fees just to survive the week. Delivery apps, rush fees, and quick fixes. Psychologists call this scarcity vision. You focus so hard on today's fire that you burn tomorrow's cash.

Take credit cards. A $3,000 balance at 24% APR paid only by the minimum takes 183 months to clear. That minimum trap racks up $4,887 in interest alone. You pay for the time you don't have. But here's the part nobody mentions. You can break the cycle by changing how you buy back hours. Instead of paying high fees for instant relief, automate one fixed transfer every single payday.

A $100 monthly investment at 8% a year compounds to $58,902 over 20 years. That's real leverage. Stop paying the scarcity tax on your survival. Free up your future self today. Follow for the money rules school never taught you.
