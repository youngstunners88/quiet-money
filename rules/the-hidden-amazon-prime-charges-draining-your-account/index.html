The full story

You're likely paying Amazon $15 a month for channels you haven't watched in over a year. Amazon buries these video add-on subscriptions deep inside menus where you'll never see them. Most people completely forget they signed up for free trials that quietly turned into paid monthly fees. Let's hunt them down right now in under 2 minutes.

Step one. Open the Amazon app on your phone or log into the desktop site right now. Step two. Tap your profile icon at the bottom, then tap your account settings. Step three. Scroll down to memberships and subscriptions, then open your Prime video channels list. Look closely at that list. You'll probably find 2 or 3 streaming add-ons you completely forgot about.

Cancel every single one of them today. You don't need them and you certainly aren't watching them. A $15 a month subscription for 10 years equals $1,800 straight out of your pocket. If you invested that $15 a month instead, historical average market returns could turn it into a solid chunk of cash. Stop letting hidden corporate fees rob your future.
