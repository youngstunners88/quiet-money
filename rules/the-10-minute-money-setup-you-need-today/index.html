The full story

Most people save whatever's left at the end of the month. That's why they have nothing left. Here's the 10-minute fix that flips the script and forces your wealth to grow on autopilot. Step one. Log into your main checking account right now. Set up a recurring automatic transfer for the day after payday.

Send that cash straight to a separate high-yield savings account you can't easily touch. Start small if you need to. Even $100 a month adds up fast. At 8% a year with monthly compounding, that $100 turns into $58,902 in 20 years. Bump it to $500 a month, and that same 20 years yields $294,510.

But here's the part nobody mentions. If you wait just 10 years, the math gets brutal. Starting at 25 versus 35 with $200 a month at 8% until 65 changes everything. That 10-year delay costs you $400,130 in missed growth. You don't need more willpower. You just need a system that runs without you.
