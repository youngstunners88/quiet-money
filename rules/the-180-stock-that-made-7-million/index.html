The $180 Stock That Made $7 Million

The true story of Grace Groner, a secretary who turned a $180 investment into $7 million through seventy-five years of pure patience.

The rule: She bought one stock in 1935.

The full story

She spent $180 on three shares of stock in 1935 and never sold a single one. Her name was Grace Groner. She worked as a secretary in Lake Forest for her entire career. She lived in a modest one-room apartment and bought her clothes at garage sales. Nobody around her knew she was hiding a massive fortune right under their noses.

Those 3 original shares split over and over again across seven decades. By the time she passed away at age 100, that tiny purchase ballooned. Her portfolio hit $7 million. But here's the part nobody mentions. She didn't pick hot stocks or time the market highs and lows.

She simply bought a great business and refused to let panic sell it. Time did the heavy lifting while she lived a quiet life on her terms. Follow for the money rules school never taught you about how
