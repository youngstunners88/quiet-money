Why viewing your emergency fund as a wasted investment opens you up to high-interest debt spirals. Keep cash to protect your future.

The full story

You think that cash sitting in your savings account is wasted money because it's not earning high returns. That's completely wrong. It's not an investment. It's insurance against a debt spiral. Here's what happens when a sudden car repair or medical bill hits an empty bank account. Most people put that $6,000 bill on a credit card running at 24% APR.
