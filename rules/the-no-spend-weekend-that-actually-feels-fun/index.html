The full story

A no-spend weekend doesn't mean sitting in an empty room staring at the wall. It's a game to find out how much cash you can keep in your account by Monday morning. Step one. Clean out your pantry tonight and plan every meal using only what's already inside. That single move stops the sneaky delivery apps from eating your paycheck.

Step two. Swap expensive weekend outings for a library card and a public park walk. Remember, a $5/day habit turns into $1,825 a year that could be working for you. Step three. Freeze your debit card in a cup of water in the freezer before you sleep. If you want to spend money on Saturday, you've got to wait for the ice to melt.

That simple friction kills impulse buys dead in their tracks. Just like a $3,000 credit card balance at 24% APR takes years to clear out if you only pay the minimum...
