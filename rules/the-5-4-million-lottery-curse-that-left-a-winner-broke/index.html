The full story

She beat 1-in-17-trillion odds twice. Then she lost it all. In 1985 and 1986, Evelyn Adams won the New Jersey lottery twice, totaling $5.4 million. Most people think a giant cash windfall solves every financial problem you'll ever face. It doesn't. Because money doesn't fix a broken relationship with risk.

If your default behavior is speculative, extra capital just speeds up your losses. Evelyn didn't invest in safe assets or build a lasting safety net for her future. She headed straight to Atlantic City. She gambled constantly and gave cash away to everyone. Within years, every single dollar vanished into thin air without a trace.

Here's the brutal truth nobody mentions in those glossy winner interviews. More money in reckless hands just accelerates the crash into total financial ruin. She ended up living in a modest trailer park, broke and out of options. Fix your habits before you wish for a bigger bank account.
