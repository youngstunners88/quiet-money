Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-23. This explains what information Quiet Money collects through this website, and what it does not.

What we collect

  • Website visits. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) under its own privacy policy. We do not run an analytics tag or advertising tracker on this site.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when you open a page.
  • Videos. Embedded videos come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed, which does not set cookies until you press play.
  • Email (when offered). If we open an email list, you will give your email address on a sign-up form. We will use it only to send the newsletter you asked for and you can unsubscribe at any time. This form is not active on the site yet.
  • Purchases. Paid products are sold through third-party platforms. Those platforms collect the payment and purchase details under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your card details.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, do not have user accounts on this site, and do not ask for passwords or payment details here.

Social platforms

When you follow or comment on our videos on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, that platform handles your data under its own policy. We see only the public comments and counts those platforms show us.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Your choices

You can block cookies and third-party requests in your browser, unsubscribe from any future email, and ask us to delete any personal information we hold about you.

Contact

To ask about your data or this policy, open an issue on our public repository.
