## Formats, ranked for Quiet Money (all U until watched)
1. **Daily saving-habit loop** (savingjourney_daily: 13 s, about 97k likes). Very short, repeatable, payoff in seconds. Weakness: our videos are 61 to 72 s, so this is a second format, not a swap.
2. **Single-idea money psychology at 55 to 65 s** (moneyinmono, golgomalongo). Same length as our videos and the strongest proof the length is not a barrier. This is out-execute territory.
3. **"What actually happens" consequence explainers** (consequence.meter, 328 followers). Strong ratio, but the topic is legal edges (cash structuring). Reject for us: it sits close to advice and to wrongdoing (rule 2).

## Not confirmed
The 2026-11-08 scan said silent face with on-screen rules was winning in money. This data does not show it: the best small-account money posts are habit loops and spoken-or-captioned explainers. Treat "silent-face rules" as a test, not a finding.
