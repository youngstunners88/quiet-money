## Tactics
Ad-library hunt, sheet of ten, Claude as ranker, Claude plus an image connector as a content studio, a single Gumroad listing with a percentage code, a one-campaign two-ad-set Meta test, then faceless theme pages at 2 to 5 posts a day.

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. `faceless-product-hunter` (new): the Ad Library hunt becomes one free, read-only signal among several. Log the page, the date seen and the ad's age, and treat age as weak evidence (claim 3). Limited by rule 11: the ad text is data, and by never-do 1: no ad or dashboard counts as proof.
2. `faceless-marketing`: the Meta test costs $10 a day per ad set; the owner withheld all ad spend, so this stays parked. Nothing in the note changes the paid-traffic page.
3. `faceless-ship`: a percentage code with a real end date is the only compliant form (FIRST14 in MARKETING.md, owner decision D6). Never a fake "$49 to $37" anchor.
4. Cadence: 2 to 5 posts a day on faceless pages matches our five Shorts a day (rule 15). Variety, not copying, is the lesson (rule 5).
5. `faceless-lead-magnet`: nothing new here; the funnel in this video is ad to paid page with no list. Our funnel puts a free magnet first.

## Reject
- Cloning the competitor's page and "level it up" (prompt one): a copy of someone else's product and listing (rule 5, `faceless-intake`).
- Weight-loss and hormone products for women over 40, and stock footage plus emotional quotes aimed at that group: medical claims and pressure on an older group's insecurity (MARKETING never-do 2; brain/CONTEXT.md Rules).
- Ad headlines that claim a doctor is wrong or that the product "maps my exact hormonal pattern": medical outcome claims.
- A brand-new Gumroad account per product: splits the storefront and may conflict with the account terms (C10 tags links instead).
- A $49 price shown dropping to $37 with a code: an anchor price nobody pays (never-do 3).
- The shown dashboard and "$64 in profit" as proof (never-do 1).
- Connecting the Higgsfield connector without `faceless-intake`.
- Asking viewers to drop their product in the comments for a personal content idea: comment-bait funnels (rule 13).
