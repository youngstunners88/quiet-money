# The $100M Scaling Roadmap, Stage 1: Monetize

## In one line
A business moves through named stages; the first two are getting people to try it free, then making the first paid sales again and again.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Label | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ten stages: Improvise, Monetize, Advertise, Stabilize, Prioritize, Productize, Optimize, Categorize, Specialize, Capitalize | S | |
| 2 | Stage 0 graduates when people use your free thing and find it helpful | S | |
| 3 | Stage 1 graduates with consistent first sales to different customers, not one sale | S | |
| 4 | Stage 1's main constraint is the product: a "Version 1" good enough to pay for | S | |
| 5 | Stage 2: 100 minutes of marketing a day; double down on one channel before diversifying | S | his Rule of 100 variant |
| 6 | About 30 million people reach this stage; 9% of people in the US own a business | U | figures not sourced in the PDF |

## Tactics
Name your stage, fix only that stage's constraint, and graduate on a clear test (first consistent sales, then consistent new customers).

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. channel/empire: we are at Stage 1 (live product, 0 sales); the graduation test is consistent first sales. The constraint list maps
   to us: proof from free users (none yet), telling people we charge (videos), tracking who paid (shop sales).
2. Escape pillar: "which stage is your side business in?" as a self-check video, with the honest catch that most new businesses close.
3. faceless-ceo: use the stage as the sprint frame; do not plan Stage 3 work (stabilize, hire) before Stage 1 is passed.

## Reject
- The Skool ads on every stage page (an affiliate push, not a lesson).
- The unsourced population figures (claim 6) as facts.

## Open questions or tests
None; a planning frame, not a claim to test.

## Evidence
- "graduate by making the first sale, then consistent first sales to different customers."
