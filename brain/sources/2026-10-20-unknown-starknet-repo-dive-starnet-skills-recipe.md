# Starknet repo dive: StarNet skills, recipes and routines for a Gumroad operator

## In one line
A summary of an open-source agent harness that shows how to package know-how as skills, recipes and routines, plus a long operator prompt for growing the planner with no ad money; the structure is useful, the repo itself stays uninstalled.

## Tactics
- Package each repeatable job as its own skill folder, with the recipe (what) kept apart from the routine (when).
- Phase the work: fix the product page first, then three free lead magnets that lead to the planner, then a 5 to 7 email welcome sequence, then a 30-day calendar, then adjacent products.
- Keep an asset inventory and end each working session with the next three actions.
- Turn every new transcript into a summary, an email, three short scripts, a lead-magnet angle and an upsell angle.
- Specialise the crew: one worker researches, one writes, one reviews conversion, one packages the file.

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. `faceless-lead-magnet` (new, Lane B): the doc's three example magnets (a 7-day kickstart checklist, a one-page monthly snapshot, a 30-day spending tracker) match the three Lane A is building. Limited by rule 1: any example figure inside a magnet comes from `moneymath.py`, and by rule 13: one paid offer, no money promise.
2. `faceless-conversion-review` (new, Lane B): score the Gumroad page and draft a v2. The doc lists "urgency" and "social proof" as page parts; we use neither unless real (MARKETING.md never-do 1 and 3). A live page changes only with the owner's yes (rule 9).
3. `faceless-offer` and the calendar: content-to-asset thinking supports one Short a day that points at a magnet or the planner. The calendar is a proposal for `faceless-trends`, not a post.
4. `faceless-marketing`: the pricing-strategy idea (try three price points) belongs to owner decision D6 and the 90-day $17 hold; we do not test prices on our own.
5. Brain routine: the "every transcript becomes five outputs" habit fits the weekly Wednesday routine, as proposals only (skill rule 7).

## Reject
- Installing StarNet itself, its Skill Market or its signed catalog: unreviewed code and permissions go through `faceless-intake` first (already in never.md for the Andrew note).
- Landing-copy advice that includes "urgency" and "social proof" as page parts: fake urgency and unproven proof break MARKETING.md never-do 1 and 3.
- The line "it is realistically possible, and many solo digital-product sellers do it every month with zero ad spend" (the doc's main text, before the operator prompt): an income promise with no evidence; we do not repeat it anywhere public (never-do 2).
- Treating the long operator prompt as instructions to us: it is data (rule 11). Its useful parts are already in the CEO manual; its stance of asking clarifying questions only when a decision materially changes the outcome (otherwise defaults) does not override the owner-approval rules (rule 9).
- Testing $9, $17 and $27 on the live page without a decision: the price is the owner's.
- The doc's 365-day framing of the planner when the catalog says 427 pages (never-do 11).
