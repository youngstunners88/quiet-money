## Tactics
A skill that runs a repeatable research pipeline from one short prompt; a brand kit before any image; a store built from a reference page; ad scripts based on competitor ads; building the product after the order.

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. `faceless-product-hunter` (new): borrow the shape, not the content. One short kickoff prompt, a phased pipeline, a ranked handoff of three candidates, and the rule to read every phase. Our version scores proof from free and capped sources (rule 7) and never starts a build (MARKETING.md: never sell what is not built).
2. `faceless-conversion-review` (new): the "make the page better for conversions" step is a review of our own listing against our own rubric; a v2 is drafted and the live page changes only with the owner's yes (rule 9).
3. `faceless-ship` and `faceless-empire`: nothing to adopt. We already have the product built before it is listed (the planner is 427 pages in MARKETING.md), and our storefront is Gumroad plus Etsy, not a new Shopify shop.
4. `topics/tools.md`: record the spy-tool and store-builder stack as watched, not adopted; every tool goes through `faceless-intake`.
5. Pricing: the "$29 minimum" claim does not change the planner's $17, which the owner approved; the claim is logged as contested in `topics/product-and-pricing.md`.

## Reject
- Selling a product that does not exist yet and building it "once someone buys": a fake door, taking money for something not delivered (MARKETING.md "never sell what is not built"; ORCHESTRATOR.md section 2).
- An invented first-person testimonial ("My doctor told me I'm too young", "She actually listened") presented as a real customer: fake proof (MARKETING never-do 1) and an AI person posing as a customer (never-do 5).
- Menopause, trauma and ADHD products pitched at people who feel dismissed by their doctor: medical claims and targeted pressure (never-do 2; brain/CONTEXT.md Rules).
- Cloning a competitor's store, images, ads and scripts, including frame-by-frame recreation: IP and originality (rule 5, `faceless-intake`).
- The unverified spy-tool revenue estimate and "$10K to $100K a month" as proof (never-do 1, 2).
- Affiliate-driven tool stacks (Shopify link, domain code, SMS sponsor): the recommendations are paid for by the vendor; we take no advice that depends on them.
- Giving a skills folder in return for likes: engagement bait and unreviewed code (rule 11, `faceless-intake`).
- "Act without asking" mode with always-allow on every connector: it removes the permission checks we depend on (rules 9 and 10).
- A floor price of $29 stated as a fact (see claim 14).
