# The New Secret Chapter (free, discount and premium promotions)

## In one line
Wrap one core offer in a free or deep-discount front end to get people in, earn on what follows, and add friction only once flow exists.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Label | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get flow, monetize flow, then add friction | S | his operating order |
| 2 | Premium, free and discount are wrappers around one core offer, not different offers | S | |
| 3 | Free gets the most responses and the cheapest leads | S | widely held; his reasoning |
| 4 | Small discounts (5-25%) rarely change behaviour; only 50%+ does | S | his opinion |
| 5 | Split tests in ten markets: free and non-free leads closed at the same rate, free cut lead cost about 5x | U | his own report, no data shown |
| 6 | The "penny gap": far more people take a free item than a one-cent one | U | he cites Dan Ariely; the 9x figure is his retelling |
| 7 | His first free offer to gyms made his first million outside his gyms (33 gyms, 18 months) | U | self-reported |
| 8 | Friction (qualifications, questions, steps, forced viewing, longer ads) cuts volume and raises quality | S | |

## Tactics
A free first step that delivers real value; then one paid step; discount a piece of the offer, never the whole thing; use
friction to sort serious buyers once volume is high.

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. faceless-offer / faceless-lead-magnet: we already run this shape without ads: the free First Quiet Week sample (flow) leads to
   the $17 planner (monetize). Keep the planner's price; do not add a discount code (MARKETING D6; Gumroad urgency rules).
2. faceless-script, escape pillar: a teachable framework for viewers who sell something ("the free first step"), told as a general
   business idea in our words, with the honest catch that free work costs time and money.
3. Measurement: the claim to test is ours, not his: does the free sample raise planner sales? It needs the visit counter (owner queue U09).

## Reject
- Working "for free in exchange for testimonials" as a script angle that implies results; and any testimonial we did not receive (never.md).
- "Forced consumption" (making people watch a long video before the button): conflicts with our calm brand.
- His split-test and million-dollar figures as facts (claims 5-7 stay U; say "he says").
- Discount codes that pressure a decision (fake urgency).

## Open questions or tests
Does a free 9-page sample move planner sales at our traffic? Needs page-visit measurement first.

## Evidence
- "Get flow, monetize flow, then add friction."
- "Promotions are wrappers around one core offer: premium (the offer alone), free, discount."
