## How Quiet Money could use it
1. faceless-empire / faceless-monid: the Meta Ad Library (and TokConnect's EU/UK ad library tools) is free, read-only
   demand research for money planners, debt trackers and budget sheets. Log long-running ads with the date seen.
   Weak signal; pair it with Monid and Etsy data.
2. faceless-marketing: the $10 question. His cost per sale was $60 / 3 = $20. Our planner is $17, about $14 net
   (MARKETING.md section 10, assumed), so about $6 lost per sale, and $10 buys about half a sale. MARKETING.md also
   requires 4-5%+ organic page conversion on 100+ visits and the owner raising the $3 ceiling before any paid test.
   Proposal: no Meta ads now (hypothesis; one anecdote, different product and price).
3. topics/paid-traffic.md: keep the ad-set template (interest vs broad, 3-5 creatives, kill the loser early) for
   a later, owner-approved test.
4. faceless-ship: Gumroad codes plus the "only if a discount is available" checkout field. The compliant version
   is a real launch code with a real end date (FIRST14 in MARKETING.md, owner approval).
5. Cadence: 2-5 posts a day matches our 5 a day; the owner's TikTok/Reels trial (C8) tests cross-posting.

## Reject
- Weight-loss and health products, and fear hooks aimed at women over 40 ("Your doctor isn't lying to you..."):
  medical claims, outside our lane, exploiting an older group's insecurity.
- Cloning a proven competitor product and "levelling it up".
- A new Gumroad account per product: splits our one storefront and may conflict with Gumroad's account terms
  (unchecked). We tag links instead (MARKETING.md C10).
- A list price nobody pays, used as an anchor: a fake compare-at price (never-do 3).
- "Just follow Claude's lead" on audiences without data.
- Connecting the Higgsfield MCP, or any connector, without faceless-intake.
- Quotes meant to "hit them emotionally" aimed at a targeted vulnerable group.
- The "$64 profit" framing.
