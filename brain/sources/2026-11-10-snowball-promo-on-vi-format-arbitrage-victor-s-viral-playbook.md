# Format arbitrage: Victor's viral playbook

## In one line
Treat short video as a market: find a format that many people watch and few people make, then out-execute it, import it from another niche, or hand it to many creators before supply catches up.

## Tactics
Scan for outliers by views divided by followers. Name the format in one line. Pick one of three moves: out-execute, import, scale. Re-check for saturation weekly.

## How Quiet Money could use it
1. analytics / faceless-trends: a weekly outlier scan with Monid (capped), ranked by views per follower. The first run is in `brain/concepts/format-arbitrage.md`. Limit: public numbers only, rule 1.
2. faceless-script: import a format into money psychology, in our own words and with our own facts. Limit: rule 2 (education, no advice) and rule 3 (no real faces).
3. Scale with creators stays parked: it needs other people posting under our brand, which is the owner's call (rule 9).

## Reject
- Buying or signing up for Snowball: nothing in the pitch beats the Monid desk we already have, and the sign-up path is a comment funnel.
- Copying another creator's script or footage. We copy a structure, never the words.
- Accounts made only to farm a format ("fresh accounts"): spreads the brand thin and risks platform spam rules.

## Open questions or tests
- Does a high views-per-follower ratio repeat for the same account, or is it one lucky post? Check each outlier account's last 5 posts before calling a format real.
- Does the format hold for 61 to 72 second money videos, or only for short loops?
