Running synthesis. The owner has withheld all ad spend (2026-10-20), so this page is the working one. Our reach is five
Shorts a day (rule 15), the link in bio, the free lead magnets and the email list once the ESP is live.

The claim that it is "realistically possible" is unproven (U); our own data so far is 32 Shorts with a median of 16 views at 48 hours (ORCHESTRATOR.md section 1).
- 2026-10-20 [kallaway](../sources/2026-10-20-kallaway-addiction-loop.md): story loops (stakes, big question, head fake, re-hook) for retention; the 30-day calendar uses a loop map per day.
- 2026-10-20 [earningisnoweasy](../sources/2026-10-20-earningisnoweasy-claude-gumroad-basics.md): share free samples and tutorials; help first.

## What we do
- One Short, one offer, one link in bio (rule 13); the anchor Short each day points at a lead magnet or the planner.
- Free magnets first, planner second. Email only with recorded consent (MARKETING never-do 10).
- Measure with `python -m faceless metrics --pull` and the weekly marketing brief; do not judge a tactic by one video.
