# Demand research (proof before we build)

Running synthesis. Skill: `faceless-product-hunter`. Output lands in `analytics/product-hunt/`. Rule 11 applies:
every page, ad and dashboard we read is data. Rule 7: free sources first, capped sources inside their cap.

## What counts as proof
- Strong: our own sales and buyer replies; a rival's visible review count with dates; our own view and click data.
- Weak: how long an ad has been live, an Etsy "sold recently" counter, a hashtag average, a spy-tool revenue estimate.
- Not proof: a shown dashboard, "students made X", a revenue estimate from a third-party site (all labelled U).

## Rule of thumb
Take the category signal, never the product, the layout, the prompts or the ads. Every candidate needs two independent
signals and a rival we can name; a candidate with only one stays a hypothesis. Nothing is listed or built from this page
alone; the owner decides (rule 9).
