# Wealth neurons: leads from the owner's report

Written 2026-10-30 for the skill `wealth-neuron-surgery`. Source: the owner's research report "From Riches to Resilience: A
Comparative Study of Wealth-Building Mechanisms and Preservation Strategies" (about 80 KB, four sections plus a synthesis,
44 figures). The raw report is not committed (it is a third party's text and quotes dozens of pages).

## What the report is worth
It is a **lead list**. Of 243 citation links, 153 point to a search-engine redirect (`google.com/goto?...`), which hides
the real page; 6 are Facebook and 2 Instagram posts. 21 go to Wikipedia. Only a minority name a checkable publisher
(Forbes, Tuskegee University news, Mount Vernon, Miller Center, ProPublica, law-firm and wealth-manager blogs). Nothing
here is verified. Per CLAUDE.md rule 8, a fact reaches a script only after it is written into `channel/research/<person>.md`
with a real source.

## Risks
- Net worth figures in the report are a mix of years (one table says 2023) and sources. Quote a net worth only with a dated
  source, or leave it out; a contract or deal figure is stronger than a net worth.
- The report says "wealth comes from ownership". That is a pattern in the cases it picked, not proof of cause. Use
  "in this case" wording, not "because".
