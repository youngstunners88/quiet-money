## Summary
Short video behaves like a market. A format that many people watch and few make gives small accounts big views. The moves are: out-execute it, import it from another niche, or (later, with the owner's yes) hand it to several creators. The edge closes as copies appear.

## Mechanism
Signal is views divided by followers on recent posts. A tiny account with a high ratio means the format, not the creator, did the work. Confirm with the account's last five posts before believing it.

## Accessibility
Free to run. Monid scan costs about $0.0024 per four queries. No new tool needed.

## Numbers and evidence
Measured 2026-11-10 (analytics/format-scans/2026-11-10.md): 117 TikTok videos in 4 searches; clearest outliers are 266 to 35k-follower accounts with 123x to 247x views per follower. Victor's own figures (2B views, 70M in 100 videos) are U.
