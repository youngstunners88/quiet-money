3/7 Most people assume they need a higher income. But you actually just need a simple sinking fund. Here's the 15-minute system to neutralize every yearly expense before it ever hits your mailbox. Step one.

---

4/7 Choose one recurring bill due later this year, like car registration or holiday gifts. Take the total dollar amount and count the exact months remaining until that bill is due. Step two.

---

5/7 Divide that total cost by the remaining months to calculate your exact monthly requirement. Divide that figure by your paychecks per month so you know what to save each payday. Step three.

---

6/7 Open a separate sub-account and automate that exact transfer on every single payday. When the invoice lands, the full balance is already sitting there, completely paid for without stress.
