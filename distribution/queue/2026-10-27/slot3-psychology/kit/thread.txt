1/6 Why 'Free' Makes You Spend $50 Extra

The free shipping trap

---

2/6 Slap on free shipping for a $70 item you don't need, and you buy it immediately. Most people think they're just saving $5 and getting a smart deal. But researchers call this the Zero-Price Effect, where zero turns off your brain's fear of loss.

---

3/6 In a famous MIT experiment, people picked cheap free chocolate over a luxury truffle at a massive discount. When something costs zero, your brain overvalues the item and ignores the hidden trade-off.

---

4/6 Online stores exploit this by forcing you to spend $50 extra just to dodge a $5 delivery charge. What does wasting $5 a day on useless impulse add-ons actually cost your wealth? A $5 a day habit equals $1,825 a year leaving your wallet.

---
