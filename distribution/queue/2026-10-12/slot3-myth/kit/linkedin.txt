The Mortgage Payoff Trap

Paying off your mortgage early is a trap.

That fixed rate debt is cheap. Meanwhile, missing out on compounding interest destroys your net worth.

Take investing $500 a month at 8% a year. In 30 years, it grows into $745,180.

If you send that money to the bank instead, you save a tiny 3% or 4% interest rate. You're trading a massive compounding return for a tiny guaranteed savings.

Look at the math. In 40 years, that same $500 a month turns into $1,745,504.

That house is paid off, but your overall wealth is miles behind where it could be. Inflation also eats away at that debt.
