Write Your Money Rules On One Index Card

Complex financial plans always fail.

Here's the fix. You can fit every rule you actually need on a single index card.

Step one. Grab a blank card and write down your absolute maximum savings rate.

For instance, investing $500 a month at 8% a year builds $745,180 in 30 years. Step two.

Write down your hard limits on credit card debt and impulse shopping. Carrying a $6,000 balance at 24% APR paying only the minimum takes 252 months to clear.

That single habit costs you $10,887 in interest alone. But here's the part nobody mentions.

Try this: When a money choice pops up, check the card before you spend a single dollar.
