The 0% Interest Trap That Back-Charges Your Purchase

That 0% deal has a hidden catch.

Here is the trap they hide in the fine print. Miss the payoff deadline by one single day.

You do not just pay interest on what is left. They back-charge it on the full original purchase price.

Imagine buying a $6,000 credit card balance or a large appliance on deferred terms. You pay down most of it.

Only $100 remains when the promo period ends. Because of that tiny leftover sum, retroactive interest triggers at rates like 24% on the entire original amount.

At 24%, debt doubles every 3 years according to the rule of 72. That mistake costs you dearly.
