1/7 Why Your Next Raise Won't Set You Free

Why raises won't set you free

---

2/7 You get a $5,000 raise, but your bank account feels just as tight six months later. So why does every paycheck boost disappear before it ever sets you free? Most people think earning more money is the fix.

---

3/7 But fear keeps you in the chair, and desire spends the extra cash immediately. That's called lifestyle creep, and it turns every new salary into a higher baseline expense.

---

4/7 To break out, your pay must buy assets that put cash back into your pocket every month. If you invest $500 a month at 8% a year, you reach $600,000 in 27.6 years. But how much passive income does that actually generate when you want to stop working?

---

5/7 Under the 4% rule, that $600,000 portfolio generates $2,000 a month in passive income. Here's the honest catch: building passive assets takes years of discipline and carries real market risks.

---

6/7 This week, audit your last raise and route half of every future bump into investments. Earning more won't buy freedom until you stop letting emotion direct every dollar.
