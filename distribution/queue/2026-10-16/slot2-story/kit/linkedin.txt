How a 19-Year-Old Blew £9.7 Million in 8 Years

But here's the part nobody mentions: the money didn't vanish on reckless driving. It vanished because sudden wealth acts like an accelerant when capital arrives before financial discipline.

Without social boundaries, he handed out £4 million to friends and hangers-on who quickly disappeared. By 2010, the parties stopped, the mansion was condemned, and his bank account hit zero.
