1/7 The Raise Math Nobody Teaches You

Why a raise feels smaller

---

2/7 People think crossing into a higher tax bracket means losing money on your entire salary. That is completely false. Marginal brackets only apply to the dollars sitting above that specific income line.

---

3/7 Marginal tax brackets mean you always take home more when you earn more, but supplemental withholding on bonuses can temporarily take a bigger bite upfront.

---

4/7 When your employer pays a bonus, federal rules often mandate a flat twenty-two percent withholding rate. If that flat rate sits higher than your normal tax bracket, your net payout looks noticeably lighter than expected.

---

5/7 You don't lose that money permanently—any over-withholding comes back as a tax refund when you file. Understanding this cash flow dip stops you from panicking when your annual bonus hits your account.

---

6/7 Always check your yearly tax return to ensure your total withholding matches your actual liability. Mastering the mechanics of your paycheck keeps you in control of your financial growth.
