1/6 Write Your Asset Column in 10 Minutes

Name one thing that pays you

---

2/6 Your paycheck funds today, but what actually funds your life when you stop working? Most think their house or car is an asset, but if it drains cash, it's a liability. So how do you build an asset column that pays you, starting in ten minutes?

---

3/6 Step one. Open a blank note on your phone titled My Asset Column. List everything you own that deposits cash into your bank each month, even if it's $0. Step two. Add one target line item, like $100 a month into an 8% index fund.

---

4/6 What does writing down a tiny $100 deposit actually build over time? At 8% a year, $100 a month invested for 30 years becomes $149,036 from $36,000 put in. Step three. Automate that transfer right now so your asset column grows on its own.

---

5/6 Real talk: passive income takes years of deliberate work, not overnight magic. Once your asset column pays your monthly bills, you don't need a job to survive.
