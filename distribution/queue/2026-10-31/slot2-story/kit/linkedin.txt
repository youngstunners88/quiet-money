The $35 Billion Illusion: How Eike Batista Lost It All

From $35 billion to $1b debt.

He was the world's seventh-richest man, publicly vowing to overtake Bill Gates as number one. How does a $35 billion fortune evaporate into negative net worth in under two years?

Most assume a sudden stock market crash or catastrophic legal fine wiped him out. That's not what happened.

When production started, actual oil yield was a fraction of what he promised investors. Stock prices plummeted 99%, triggering billions in debt calls he couldn't cover with real cash.
