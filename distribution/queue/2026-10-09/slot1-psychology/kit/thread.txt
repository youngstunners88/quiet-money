1/7 Why Your Cards Keep You Broke

The card trap

---

2/7 A famous MIT study proved card buyers willingly bid up to 100% more than cash buyers. When you hold physical paper cash, parting with it triggers mild, actual discomfort in your mind.

---

3/7 Psychologists call this the pain of paying, and it's your natural, biological spending brake. But modern credit cards and digital wallets completely bypass this biological friction.

---

4/7 But here's the part nobody mentions: the easier it is to pay, the less you care. Features like Apple Pay and face scans make parting with your money entirely invisible.

---

5/7 You're not careless; your brain simply doesn't register that you've lost anything at all. To protect your wealth, you must build the transaction friction back manually.

---

6/7 Step one: delete all saved credit card details from your phone and favorite shopping apps. Step two: set up immediate phone alerts for every single transaction, no matter how small. Because if you don't feel the pain of paying, you'll never stop
