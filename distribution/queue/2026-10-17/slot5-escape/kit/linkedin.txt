Why Your Paycheck Carries the Highest Tax

The tax code punishes your paycheck.

If you trade hours for wages, you're trapped on the most penalized side of the economy. Author Robert Kiyosaki mapped this out: employees and contractors trade time, while business owners and investors own systems.

On the left side, ordinary income faces income taxes plus payroll taxes before you see a single dollar. On the right side, investors own assets that grow untaxed for decades, taxed later at lower capital gains rates.

But here's the part nobody mentions: you can't escape this penalty just by working longer hours. More overtime just pushes your next hard-earned dollar into a higher tax bracket.

Real escape requires assets. To replace a paycheck with $2,000/month of passive income needs about $600,000 invested.

By investing $500/month at 8%/yr, you reach that $600,000 in 27.6 years. Make one move this week: pay yourself first by routing money into investments before paying any bills.
