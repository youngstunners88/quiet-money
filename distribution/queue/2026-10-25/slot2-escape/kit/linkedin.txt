The Net Cash Flow Test for Paycheck Independence

If an investment drains your cash, it delays retirement.

Most people assume any asset that gains value on paper is building their path to freedom. That's a trap.

A rental property or side project that drains cash flow each month is a liability in disguise. So how do you separate true yield-generating assets from high-maintenance financial burdens?

Let's run the net cash flow test on your portfolio right now. Add up every dollar of monthly income an asset produces.

Now subtract every single dollar of maintenance, debt service, and management fees. If that final number is negative, it's not an asset.

It's an expense draining your paycheck. Real passive income requires assets that put cash in your pocket every month without extra effort.
