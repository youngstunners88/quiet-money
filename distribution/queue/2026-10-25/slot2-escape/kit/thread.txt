1/7 The Net Cash Flow Test for Paycheck Independence

If an investment drains your cash, it delays retirement

---

2/7 Most people assume any asset that gains value on paper is building their path to freedom. That's a trap. A rental property or side project that drains cash flow each month is a liability in disguise.

---

3/7 So how do you separate true yield-generating assets from high-maintenance financial burdens? Let's run the net cash flow test on your portfolio right now. Add up every dollar of monthly income an asset produces.

---

4/7 Now subtract every single dollar of maintenance, debt service, and management fees. If that final number is negative, it's not an asset. It's an expense draining your paycheck.

---

5/7 Real passive income requires assets that put cash in your pocket every month without extra effort. For instance, generating $2,000 a month of passive income needs about $600,000 invested.

---

6/7 Run the net cash flow test on your holdings this week and cut the dead weight.
