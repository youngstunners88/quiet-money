The Cashflow Quadrant Shift: Moving to Passive Income

Trading hours kills wealth.

Here is the exact mathematical shift to cross the quadrant. Most people stay trapped on the left side as employees and self-employed workers.

That side forces you to trade every single hour of your life for a paycheck. Why do incomes rise while people still feel completely broke?

Because expenses climb right alongside earnings, keeping your net cash flow flat. The right side of the quadrant belongs to business owners and investors who own systems.

Assets put money in your pocket every month while liabilities take money out. How do you actually build enough passive income to cover your life?
