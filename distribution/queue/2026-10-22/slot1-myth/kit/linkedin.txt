The True Cost of Buying a House

The rent vs buy myth.

Why do we assume owning a house always beats renting an apartment over the long run? Most people think property value gains cover every hidden cost of homeownership.

Property taxes, roof repairs, and interest eat up massive chunks of your cash flow quietly. What happens when you invest that down payment difference into the stock market instead?

Investing $500 a month at 8% a year for 30 years grows to $745,180. That same $500 monthly investment over 40 years reaches $1,745,504.

How does maintenance drain your wealth compared to a simple index portfolio? An apartment keeps your capital liquid and free from unexpected repair bills.

Flexibility lets your money compound faster without being tied to one zip code. Stop trapping your cash in walls that demand constant upkeep.
