Why your loan balance is growing

2/6 You chose an income-driven repayment plan to keep your monthly bills affordable on an entry-level salary. How does a monthly payment leave your principal higher year after year?

---

3/6 Most people assume every dollar you send covers your interest first and chips away at your original debt. That is false. On income-driven plans, your monthly payment often only covers a fraction of the interest.

---

4/6 Where does all the leftover unpaid interest go every single month? It doesn't vanish. It piles on top of your starting balance in a hidden loop called negative amortization. Why does your total debt balloon even when you never miss a single due date?

---

5/6 Because your interest compounds faster than your payment reduces it. The math works against your favor. Your student loan balance grows every month because your minimum payment is smaller than the monthly interest charge.
