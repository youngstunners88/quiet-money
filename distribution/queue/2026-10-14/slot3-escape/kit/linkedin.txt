That baseline comes directly from the 4% safe withdrawal rule established in 1998. At a 4% withdrawal rate, your invested assets fund life without depleting your principal balance.

Change your monthly living budget, and that freedom number changes right alongside it. At $2,000 a month your target is $600,000; at $5,000 a month, it jumps to $1,500,000.

The reality? Building that capital takes consistent investing over decades.

Here's the part nobody mentions: you can't skip the slow math of compounding. Investing $1,000 a month at an 8% annual return reaches $900,000 in 24.4 years.

If you invest $2,000 a month at 8%, you cut that wait down to 17.4 years. The honest catch is market volatility; 4% is a historical guideline, not a contract.
