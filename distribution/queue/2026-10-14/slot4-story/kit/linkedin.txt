The $100M Fortune That Froze Its Owner

She had millions but no heat.

Her name was Hetty Green, and her massive fortune hid a quiet psychological trap. Hetty accumulated over $100,000,000 on Wall Street, worth more than $2,500,000,000 in modern purchasing power.

She rescued major banks, financed massive railroads, and outmaneuvered the most powerful men in finance. Yet she wore a single tattered black dress every day until it completely wore out.

But here's the dark truth about extreme wealth hoarding. Hetty never bought a permanent home, sleeping in cheap, freezing boarding houses to dodge taxes.

She ate cold oatmeal heated on office radiators because buying a hot lunch felt like failure. She proved that having immense capital doesn't protect you from a crippling scarcity mindset.
