Why You Keep Paying for Things You Don't Use

Why you keep wasting money.

Your brain aggressively defends that wasted monthly charge, but a simple mental shift stops the bleeding today. Economists call this behavioral trap the sunk cost fallacy.

A forgotten $15 monthly charge bleeds $1,800 over a decade without adding a cent of value. You keep paying because admitting you won't use it feels like admitting you wasted your signup fee.

But here's the part nobody mentions about how your brain calculates loss. That cash you spent yesterday is already gone, whether you keep paying or cancel the contract right now.

People hold tanking investments and untouched gym memberships just to avoid the quiet sting of being wrong. The counter-move is simple: treat every past transaction as entirely dead capital.

Ask only one question: would you buy this service today at full price? Redirecting just $100 a month into an index fund at 8% a year builds $58,902 in 20 years.
