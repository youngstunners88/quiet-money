### Why You Keep Paying for Things You Don't Use

The sunk cost fallacy tricks us into paying for unused subscriptions, empty gym memberships, and losing investments just to avoid admitting a loss. Treating past dollars as dead capital allows you to redirect leaked cash into long-term compounding assets. Audit your recurring expenses and cut what doesn't serve tomorrow.
