### The Bi-Weekly Mortgage Hack That Erases 8 Years

Learn how switching to a bi-weekly mortgage payment schedule creates 13 full monthly payments every year instead of 12. This extra principal-only payment turns a 30-year mortgage into a 22-year loan without changing your overall budget.

**The number** — 52 Weeks = 26 Pays: There are 52 weeks in a year, giving you 26 half-payments every two weeks.

**Try this:** Call your loan provider today and ask for an automated bi-weekly payment schedule.
