The Bi-Weekly Mortgage Hack That Erases 8 Years

Erase 8 years of mortgage debt.

Most homeowners pay their bill once a month, right on the first. How does splitting that exact same paycheck in half cut your loan down fast?

You assume paying bi-weekly just spreads your normal bill evenly throughout the year. But calendar math hides a powerful twist most homeowners never calculate for themselves.

There are 52 weeks in a year, giving you 26 half-payments every two weeks. 26 half-payments equal 13 full monthly payments every year, instead of just 12.

But where does that sudden 13th payment go, and how does it help you? That extra payment hits your principal directly, skipping interest costs that normally compound against you.

That single adjustment turns a 30-year loan into a 22-year loan automatically. Call your loan provider today and ask for an automated bi-weekly payment schedule.
