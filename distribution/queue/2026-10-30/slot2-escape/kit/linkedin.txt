Why Buying Cash Flow Beats Starting from Zero

Buy cash flow not zero.

Building a company from zero takes years of unpaid effort before you're seeing a single dollar. Most people assume you've got to invent something new.

But what if you bought existing profits instead? Buyers acquire established companies using debt leverage, putting down initial cash and borrowing what's left from lenders.
