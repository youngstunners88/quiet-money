Title: The Ostrich Effect: Why You Stop Checking Your Money When It Hurts

In a Review of Financial Studies paper using Vanguard data on 1.1 million investors over two years, account logins fell 9.5 percent after a decline in the previous day's stock market. Economists call avoiding bad financial news the ostrich effect. A fixed review date is one way to look anyway. Educational only, not financial advice.
