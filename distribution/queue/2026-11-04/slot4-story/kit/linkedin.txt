Why $45k Beats $150k For Early Retirement

$45k salary beats $150k salary.

Most workers believe early financial freedom requires six figures, but compounding math proves that's wrong. You'd assume earning $150,000 lets you retire quickly, while $45,000 keeps you trapped forever.

But a high earner saving 5% invests $7,500 yearly, leaving living expenses at $142,500. To support those heavy expenses, that six-figure earner's forced to work 66 years to retire.

So why does saving half of a modest $45,000 paycheck shatter that long timeline completely? Saving 50% on $45,000 cuts living costs to $22,500 while investing $22,500 every single year.

At a standard 7% return, saving half your income lets you retire in under 17 years. That's a massive 49-year gap, but how does savings rate actually matter more than income?

High spending forces a massive target nest egg, while high saving shrinks the goal post. Freedom isn't bought with a huge salary, it's bought by keeping your savings rate high.
