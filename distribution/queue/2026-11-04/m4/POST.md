Title: Closing A Credit Card Does Not Lift Your Score

Many people think closing an unused credit card cleans up their credit. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau says closing an existing card can raise your credit utilization ratio and lower your score, and that keeping an old account with a positive payment history may help maintain a higher score. Closing can still make sense if a card has an annual fee or poor terms, and the change may be temporary or minor. Educational only, not financial advice.
