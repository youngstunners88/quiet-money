How to Kill Hidden PayPal Billing Authorizations

Audit your paypal recurring charges right now.

When you bought that one-off trial years ago, you likely granted permanent automatic billing authorization. Step one.

Log into PayPal on a web browser, click the gear icon, and choose Account Settings. The phone app hides this menu, so a desktop browser or mobile web view works best.

Step two. Click Money, Banks and Cards on the left, then scroll down to Automatic Payments.

But here's the part nobody mentions. Every vendor you authorized over the years is still active.

You'll likely spot at least one or two recurring charges you forgot about years ago. A $15/month subscription forgotten for 10 years drains $1,800 straight out of your pocket.

Try this: Take 5 minutes to audit this list twice a year to protect your hard-earned cash.
