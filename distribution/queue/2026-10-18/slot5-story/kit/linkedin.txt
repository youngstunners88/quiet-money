How Nicolas Cage Blew $150 Million

The $150m celebrity money trap.

Nicolas Cage earned Hollywood fortunes, but one deadly assumption wiped out nearly everything he built. Between 1996 and 2011, Cage spent money assuming high paychecks would arrive forever.

He bought 15 mansions, including two European castles and an entire private island in the Bahamas. Then came a $276,000 dinosaur skull, rare vintage comics, and even exotic albino cobras.

But here's the part nobody mentions about collecting trophy assets. An expensive object isn't just a purchase; it's a permanent monthly bill.

Castles and islands don't produce rent; they demand massive taxes, insurance, security, and staffing. When box office hits slowed, his income dropped, but those monthly carrying costs stayed completely fixed.

By 2009, he faced over $13 million in unpaid federal tax liens. Because castles aren't liquid, he took catastrophic fire-sale losses just to stay out of prison.
