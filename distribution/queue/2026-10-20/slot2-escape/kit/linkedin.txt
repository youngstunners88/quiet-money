The Hidden Direction of Your Cash Flow

Which way does your money flow?.

If you don't track the direction, you'll work 40 years just funding other people's retirements. Most people define an asset by its price tag, but accounting rules don't build personal wealth.

Cash flow mechanics are simple: an asset puts money into your pocket every single month. A liability does the exact opposite: it silently extracts money from your pocket every 30 days.

That new car isn't just transportation; its loan, insurance, and fuel drain hundreds every month. But here's the part nobody mentions: you can reverse the flow using simple compounding math.

Putting $200 a month into assets at 8% compounding yields $298,072 after 30 years. You only put in $72,000; the remaining $226,072 was generated entirely by the asset.
