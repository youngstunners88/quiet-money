The Insurance Myth Bleeding Your Bank Account

The insurance trap you're buying into.

Most people over-insure tiny everyday items while leaving their biggest financial exposure completely unprotected. You're told more insurance equals safety, but stacking micro-policies guarantees lost wealth over time.

A $15/month gadget protection plan adds up to $1,800 over 10 years. Investing $100/month instead at 8% a year builds $149,036 over 30 years.

People happily pay extra fees on laptops, appliances, and furniture out of emotional fear. But here's the part nobody mentions about true financial disaster.

A cracked screen won't bankrupt your household, but an unpaid medical emergency or disability will. The smart money rule is simple: self-insure small, high-probability losses with an emergency savings fund.

Only transfer risk to an insurer when a disaster would wipe out your entire net worth. Cancel gadget warranties, raise your deductibles, and redirect those saved monthly premiums straight into investments.
