The Scarcity Trap That Keeps You Broke

Why being broke costs you more.

When you're constantly stressed about cash, your brain makes expensive survival choices. You pay massive convenience fees just to survive the week.

A $3,000 balance at 24% APR paid only by the minimum takes 183 months to clear. That minimum trap racks up $4,887 in interest alone.
