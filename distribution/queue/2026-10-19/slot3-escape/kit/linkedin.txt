The Asset vs Liability Cash Flow Trap

That new car keeps you broke.

Most people work for decades trading hours for paychecks, and their expenses always rise right along with their income. That is the rat race.

And the trap isn't your salary. It's what you buy with it.

An asset puts money in your pocket every single month. A liability takes it out.

Think about it. That vehicle requires insurance, gas, repairs, and parking fees just to sit there.

Every dollar spent maintaining a liability is capital stolen from your future investments. Imagine investing just $100 a month for 30 years at 8% interest with monthly compounding.
