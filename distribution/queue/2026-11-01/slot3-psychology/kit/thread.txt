1/6 The Moral Licensing Trap That Drains Your Wallet

Why saving $20 tricks you

---

2/6 You scan the grocery aisle, skip the brand name, and save $20 on your weekly food bill. That small win sparks a hidden psychological trigger in your brain called moral licensing.

---

3/6 You think you've earned a reward for being financially disciplined all week long. So what happens when you order a $40 takeout dinner right after getting home? You feel good because you saved money earlier, but where does the actual cash go?

---

4/6 Most people assume a win cancels out a loss, leaving you even on the day. It doesn't. You didn't save $20. You just subsidized a $40 splurge with your own groceries. That single night leaves you with a net loss of $20 you never planned to spend.

---

5/6 The counter-move is brutal. Never tie your savings today to a non-budgeted reward tonight. Separate your grocery budget from your treats so your brain stops playing tricks on you.
