1/6 Your Credit Limit Is Not Your Money

Your credit limit is a trap

---

2/6 When a card issuer hands you a $6,000 limit, it feels like permission to spend $6,000. Most people assume a bank only offers a limit your paycheck can easily carry. That's the head fake.

---

3/6 A credit limit isn't your safety line; it's the lender's risk cap. Lenders profit when you carry debt, so their ceiling is built to test your limits. So what actually happens if you spend that $6,000 limit and pay minimums?

---

4/6 On a $6,000 card at 24% APR, minimum payments take 21 years to clear. Even worse, that original $6,000 debt costs you $10,887 in pure interest. So how do you stop a bank's high limit from quietly draining your financial future?

---

5/6 First, ignore the bank's limit and set your own spending line from take-home pay. Second, treat your credit card strictly like debit, paying off every balance in full. Your monthly budget must be set by cash flow, never by a lender's algorithm.
