When Paper Money Broke a Country

The 1720 crash that created millionaires.

His name was John Law, and he promised paper notes would replace gold. Stay to see how it ends.

Law founded the Mississippi Company, claiming America's Louisiana territory held mountains of pure gold. Citizens rushed to swap hard coins for shares.

Stocks jumped from 500 livres to 10,000 livres. Ordinary folks became rich overnight.

Waiters bought estates, and coachmen drove past their former masters. But here's the part nobody mentions.

The gold in Louisiana didn't exist. It was an illusion.
