1/7 Plan Your Exit Before Day One

Plan your exit before you start

---

2/7 Most founders accidentally build a job, then get trapped working 70 hours a week. You might assume buyers pay top dollar for your personal hustle and hard work. That's wrong. Nobody buys a business that breaks the minute you step out the door.

---

3/7 So how do you build a company that someone will actually pay you cash to own? Step one is deciding your exit strategy before you spend your first dollar. Investor Dan Peña lists exit planning as a core rule.

---

4/7 By his account, he built a $450 million company. He says true wealth comes from making yourself replaceable, not staying indispensable. Step two is documenting every single task so anyone can run the operations without you.

---

5/7 Here's the honest catch: using borrowed money to scale fast means you owe debt during bad months. When systems generate cash without your labor, you don't just own a job, you own a sellable asset. Decide how you'll leave before day one.
