Plan Your Exit Before Day One

Plan your exit before you start.

Most founders accidentally build a job, then get trapped working 70 hours a week. You might assume buyers pay top dollar for your personal hustle and hard work.

That's wrong. Nobody buys a business that breaks the minute you step out the door.

So how do you build a company that someone will actually pay you cash to own? Step one is deciding your exit strategy before you spend your first dollar.

Investor Dan Peña lists exit planning as a core rule. By his account, he built a $450 million company.

He says true wealth comes from making yourself replaceable, not staying indispensable. Step two is documenting every single task so anyone can run the operations without you.
