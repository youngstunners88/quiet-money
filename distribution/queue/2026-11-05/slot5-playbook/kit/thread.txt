1/6 Side Hustle Money: The One Rule That Saves You

Your first dollar is a red flag

---

2/6 Mix it in and you lose the trail. Come tax time, you’re guessing. Where does that money go? How much did you really make? Most people dump it all in one account. It’s simpler, right? But the IRS doesn’t care about simple. They care about the paper.

---

3/6 Step one. Open a separate high-yield account today. Takes 5 minutes. Name it something obvious: ‘Freelance’ or ‘Etsy.’ No creative fluff. Step two. Move that first dollar into it. Then every dollar after. Now you see every cent.

---

4/6 And so does your accountant. Step three. Track it in a spreadsheet. Date, amount, project. That’s it. An extra $1,000 a month invested at 8% for 10 years? That’s $182,946. Not bad for a side gig.

---

5/6 But here’s the kicker: without the trail, you might not even know it’s there. Separate the money from day one. Then you’ll know. And you’ll grow.
