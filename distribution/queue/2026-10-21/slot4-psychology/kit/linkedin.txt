The Fake Original Price Trap That Drains Your Wallet

The fake original price trap.

It feels like a massive steal. Why does that crossed-out number make you instantly reach for your wallet?

Most people assume they are saving $60 on a good shirt. That is anchoring bias at work.

Retailers set a high fake reference point so the real price looks cheap by comparison. How much does that habit actually cost you over a single year?

If you buy three unnecessary items because they look marked down, you burn $1,825 a year. That is a $5 a day leak.

That same $5 a day invested monthly at 8% for 30 years turns into $226,659. Break the anchor by asking one simple question before you buy.

Try this: Would you pay the sale price if it had no original tag?
