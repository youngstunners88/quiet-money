The Billion-Dollar Debt Trap

The $1 billion debt trap.

But the system he used to escape the rat race has a hidden trap. He divides debt into two categories: things that pay you, and things that cost you.

Bad debt buys cars and clothes that lose value the second you swipe your card. Good debt buys assets like real estate, using other people's money to build your wealth.

This is called financial leverage, and the math looks incredibly simple on paper. But here's the part the gurus leave out: leverage works both ways.

Imagine buying a $100,000 property with $20,000 down. That's 5x leverage.

If the market goes up 20%, you double your money before fees. But if the property price drops just 20%, your entire down payment is wiped out.

Try this: Debt is a power tool, not a safety net.

Would you ever use 5x leverage to buy an asset, or is the risk too high?
