How to Negotiate Your Bills in One 10-Minute Call

They are overcharging you on purpose.

Most people silently accept this lazy tax, but one short call forces them to drop the price. Step one.

Call their main support number and immediately ask to speak with the retention department. Regular customer service agents cannot offer deep discounts, but retention agents are paid to keep you.

Step two. Use this exact script: I want to cancel because local competitors are offering cheaper rates.

But here's the part nobody mentions. You must name a real competitor in your local area.

If you try to make up a fake price, they'll call your bluff instantly and you lose. Step three.

Try this: Ask them politely to match that specific rate or process your cancellation today.
