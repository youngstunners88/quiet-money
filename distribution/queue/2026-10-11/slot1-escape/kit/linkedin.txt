The Rule of 100: How to Land Your First Clients

Why most side hustles fail.

Here's the math on entrepreneur Alex Hormozi's Rule of 100, built to land initial clients through sheer volume. The rule is straightforward: commit to 100 primary actions every single day for 100 straight days.

If you sell a service, that means sending 100 direct reach-outs each day to qualified prospects. In 100 days, you'll put out exactly 10,000 individual contacts into your market.

Even with a low 1% closing rate, that sheer volume produces 100 paying clients or customers. You can apply this to content creation by spending 100 minutes a day creating content for 100 days.

Volume beats talent every single time, but here's the part nobody mentions. This builds active income, not passive cash, and 1 in 5 US businesses close in year one.
