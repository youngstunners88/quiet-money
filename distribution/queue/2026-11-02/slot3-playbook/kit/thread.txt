1/6 The Real Cost of Every Purchase in Hours

That shirt costs half a day

---

2/6 Before you buy it, turn the price into hours of your work. Step one. Work out your exact take-home pay per hour once. Divide your monthly take-home pay by the total hours you spend working. Step two.

---

3/6 Divide the price of that new item by your actual hourly take-home rate. Is that item really worth the exact hours you spent earning it? Step three. Ask whether the thing is worth those hours of labor.

---

4/6 Most people assume a $200 purchase is just $200 from an abstract account. The truth is you traded actual living time for that specific object. Money is just stored human time sitting in your wallet.

---

5/6 When you price things in hours, impulse buys suddenly lose their shiny appeal. Calculate your last impulse buy right now and test this rule today.
