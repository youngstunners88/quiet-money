The Real Cost of Every Purchase in Hours

That shirt costs half a day.

Before you buy it, turn the price into hours of your work. Step one.

Work out your exact take-home pay per hour once. Divide your monthly take-home pay by the total hours you spend working.

Step two. Divide the price of that new item by your actual hourly take-home rate.

Is that item really worth the exact hours you spent earning it? Step three.

Ask whether the thing is worth those hours of labor. Most people assume a $200 purchase is just $200 from an abstract account.

Try this: Calculate your last impulse buy right now and test this rule today.
