Why Holding Cash Guarantees You Lose Money

Cash is not a safe asset.

Here's the exact math that destroys your purchasing power, and the simple rule to stop it. Most people keep everything in checking accounts because seeing a steady balance feels comforting.

Your account balance never drops on paper, so you feel completely protected from risk. Inflation doesn't steal your paper bills; it quietly drains what those bills can buy.

At just 3% inflation, $100 today is worth $55 in buying power after 20 years. But here's the part nobody mentions.

While you avoided market dips, you locked in a permanent 45% drop in real wealth. Cash is a tool for short-term liquidity, not a vehicle for long-term storage.

Wealthy people hold three to six months of expenses in cash, then deploy the rest. They hold broad-market index funds and productive assets that naturally outpace rising living costs.
